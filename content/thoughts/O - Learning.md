Observations on learning things. Learnings about learning.

---
- Pick up pieces, make puzzle incrementally
	- If you don't understand, don't brute force your way to understanding that one tiny aspect - just go to something related that seems more accessible to your mind in that moment, focus on that, integrate it, continue, and eventually, you'll return to the thing you were trying to understand initially, and have the tools to break it down and get it - it'll click.
- Non-linear
	- Learning is supposed to be messy. Embrace the chaos.
