title: O - Transcending conformity, faux convergence

A great challenge of our time.

- Distinguishing between non-conformity and anti-conformity (latter for sake of it, former is just independence of thought)
- Questioning whether the "done way" is the *best* way

In an increasingly globalised, interconnected world in which we each become more intimately aware of each others' existence, thoughts, cultures - where there is a cross-pollination of culture - it become ever more important to act against homogenisation and resist conformity.

As a species, I currently think one of the most important things we can aim for is a transcendence of conformity.

We are social creatures. We are conditioned to conform. I don't mean this in a conspiracy theorist matrix way. But in a way [I wrote about this somewhere in docs], we are indeed all living in a matrix - except, that matrix is constructed mentally - internally - and is specific to each of us.

Some cultures condition/select for conformity more than others. Conformity is rewarded. But also, occasionally, if done "well" and within the current Overton Window, anti-conformity (divergence) is rewarded - and to a much larger degree than conformity.

I would go as far as to argue that all mass atrocities committed throughout history, throughout the world, can be attributed to our tendency to conform. Banality of evil. Evil outcomes are more often the result of mass lemming-like behaviour than any systemic, well-considered, intentional coordination of some dastardly master plan.[[O - evil outcomes vs evil actions]]

If we foster independent thought, and as part of that, independent development of personal moralities, ethical frameworks, compasses, self-cultivated conscience, the more likely we can resist others' "compelling" moralities (and any evil actions predicated on these "moralities"), because we have already arrived at a compelling morality of our own, which we constantly refine as our understanding of the world evolves.

Then, regardless of the implications for mass atrocity, for maximal progress in general (like, actual betterment [[I - immature progress]]), fostering divergence is central to that. And this entails a non-conformity - not accepting collective conceptions of "normal", "okay", "valid" by default.
