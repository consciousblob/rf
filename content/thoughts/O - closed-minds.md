title: O - closed-minds
---

Individuals that change the world have a very fluid, flexible conception of reality, and what the "real world" is.

The likelihood that someone who defaults to "that is not realistic" or berates others for not living in the "real world" will change the world in any substantial/meaningful way, I hypothesise, is next to none.

Now, not everyone with this flexibility in the perception/conception of reality is "destined to change the world". But if they are to - if they are to become particularly/especially relevant or a particularly prominent contributor to meaningful change in the world - they *must* have this flexibility. That is, intellectual flexibility (i.e., open-mindedness) is necessary but not sufficient to become a prominent contributor.

Why am I discussing this notion of "prominent contribution" or becoming a particularly prominent provider/instigator of change? Because, generally, as (probably) non-enlightened humans, our egos play a prominent role in what role (if any) we choose to play in the world. And our egos crave importance, significance, relevance. And so, I want to play on our egos: if you do not open your mind - if you remain intellectually inflexible - you will *never* be important, significant or relevant to the world at large. If you *do* maintain an open mind, you might. The aim of appealing to our egos in this way is to try to coax us all into opening our minds - the full opening of which I believe is necessary for an optimally functioning society.

Unless you open your mind and practice flexibility in thought and your perception/conception of reality, you will be condemned to oblivion, perpetual insignificance, irrelevance, evaporating into nothingness, because you never managed to make a dint in collective consciousness.


"We want to invent the technology to enable people to fly" ~ Wright Brothers, xxxx.
"Flying 300 humans 100,00ft about the ground is completely unrealistic. Get back in the real world." ~ many unknown individuals no one cares about, pre-plane-invention.

Now, I'm not trying to argue that "substantial contribution" is the only valid thing to pursue in life and is the only measure of success in life, only meaning in life, only indication of a "good life". No. Many other valid, beautiful ways to approach life. The primary purpose of framing this so antagonistically is to trigger those individuals with the large(st) egos, who think themselves so intelligent, so above debate, that they have everything figured out and developed an understanding of the world to the extent that they feel entitled to dismiss anything that does not align with their limited worldview as "unrealistic", "stupid" and not part of the "real world" - these are the individuals with the most closed minds, who will be most triggered by such a framing. They are the targets of such discussion. This is also a means of channelling pent-up frustration toward such individuals who seem happy to squander any thought outside the realm of "reality". They are the bastions of status quo (and its deleterious aspects). And their worldview(s) must be dismantled, so we can each reconstruct reality from the resulting materials, as benefits us all.

If you want to play a role in shaping reality, you need a flexible view of what reality is. Otherwise, you will never contribute to what reality is - for yourself, and the rest of us.
