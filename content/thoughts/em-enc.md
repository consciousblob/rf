An **encountered** thought is one that a mind inputs. An **emergent** thought is one that a mind outputs, after processing inputs (plus existing thoughts embedded within the mind's [[worldview]]).

So, an emergent thought is any thought that is not a complete replication/reflection of an encountered thought.
