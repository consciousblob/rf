I've always felt inclined to unify all pursuits in life into a singular "this is what I'm doing in life" description/plan thing. I've lost count of the number of these things that have been created and forgotten and abandoned. But with each "iteration", I do seem to gain more clarity on what it is exactly I want to direct my energies toward - now, and for the rest of (my) time. So, this is the latest attempt. And, from now on, I'll try to use this [[M - appending numbers to objects (worldview version control)]] approach so I can keep track of how these "meta" life outline things evolve over time.

Some concepts I want to consider/unify:

- Divergence & convergence
	- Divergence is for generating, creating, flowing, viewing same thing from different angles, arriving at a diverse representation of something.
	- Convergence is for refining, whittling down, synthesising all views, arriving at a concise, comprehensive representation of something.
- Open (undirected) & closed (directed) mode
	- Open mode is for diverging and exploring - expansive, gathering, following random curiosities - there are no constraints, complete freedom in approach to whatever, perhaps with a minimal constraint (like, learn about X).
	- Closed mode is for converging and investigating - refining, burrowing, honing in, drilling down - it is about making concrete, visible progress in a single area/task/project.
- Breadth & depth
	- Breadth/depth is associated with open/closed mode.
	- Breadth is about exploring and developing a decent general knowledge, widening scope of things known to a mind.
	- Depth is about investigating and burrowing into a particular area/field, cultivating "expertise" and proficiency/competence in it.
- IPO
	- Inputting relates to encountering fresh data.
	- Processing relates to consolidating, understanding, conceiving, integrating inputs into worldview.
	- Outputting also relates to consolidating and enhancing understanding, with the add-on of sharing the products of that consolidation (from processing).
- Exposition & expression
	- Exposition is about "exposing" current understanding/conception of something. More in the "intellectual" realm.
	- Expression is about representing more "feely thoughts" - thoughts stemming from certain emotional (and emotionally induced mental) states/swirls. More in the "artistic" realm.
- Encountered & emergent
	- Encountered relates more to input/process - results of interthinking, of other minds externalising thoughts, which the inputting mind encounters.
	- Emergent relates more to process/output - products of a mind's intrathinking.


So, I want to develop a system for conducting myself in life that integrates all these ideas.

## 1. Collective Intelligence + Mindscaping
Collective Intelligence (will shorten to CI) refers to the cultivation of intelligence, by and throughout our species, as both a product and process. As a *product*, intelligence can be thought of as the "solid" form of useful mind substance, which is the result/output of thinking/processing; it can be thought of as meaningfully synthesised data/info/knowledge/wisdom ([[dikw]]). As a *process*, intelligence can be thought of as the more intangible, "liquid" medium through which thought is able to take form/shape, and crystallise into the intelligence *product*. This intelligence exists within and between all minds, and *CI* refers to the total sum (or perhaps *product*, if it is thought of as more than sum of its parts) of all the (1) products of individual intelligence and (2) abilities to formulate intelligence products (individual intelligence, as process) and (3) any intelligence that could be gleaned/utilised from/via the integration of these intelligences - that is, latent intelligence that is hidden/locked away until minds are sufficiently integrated, where this integration entails (enables and is enabled by) **mindscaping**.

Mindscaping refers to the building & using of digital and cognitive infrastructure necessary for facilitating/"running" collective intelligence, to foster maximally effective intrathinking and interthinking (enhancement and coordination of all thinking/processing endeavours). Digitally, this involves development of **mindware** (hardware and software for mindscaping). Cognitively, this involves all minds learning to use themselves more effectively - learning how to learn and think (better) - and how this can both enhance and be enhanced by mindware.

All of this encompasses the notion of *artificial intelligence* - AI is simply an "arm" (or perhaps "limb" to be more general), or extension/part/amplifier, of CI. TM thinks it more useful to think of and discuss AI in the context of CI - plus **AugI** (*augmented intelligence*) - to re-situate human minds in the picture, restore/empower/protect human agency and relevance (especially as AI capabilities accelerate) and emphasise that all pursuits of AI should be in service of *better*, as defined by *humans*, who of course, first need to settle what "better" even looks like.

## 2. Essentialising the Economy
This is about considering how to de-bloat the economy, to eliminate unnecessary and/or harmful (for both worker and society) work. It's about us deciding, collectively (in various aggregable units, along a gradient of local-to-global), what we consider *essential* for what we collectively deem a decent **standard of living** (SoL) that would enable/support a decent (ideally maximal) **quality of life** (QoL), and then coordinating ourselves *intelligently* based on this. The work necessary to provide this (collectively, collaboratively determined) decent SoL for all would be (possibly substantially) less than the work that is currently "performed", and so, if all (who are on board with this approach) do this work, theoretically, each individual would have to work less than is currently necessary to make the income needed to purchase basic amenities.

CI and the mindscaping will/would probably be essential for this essentialisation, in helping us to e.g. uncover commonalities in less obvious things (beyond food, water, shelter, etc.) that we would consider necessary for decent SoL, and coordinate provision efforts. Actually, having an adequately representative mindscape - as in, digispace that sufficiently represents all minds that need/want to participate in the economy - would be helpful whether or not we pursue essentialisation. In fact, TM believes/hopes that these two pursuits (of mindscaping and economy essentialisation) would self-reinforce - i.e., mindscaping would catalyse and be catalysed by our efforts to transition from meaningless means (to no end other than basic survival) work toward truly *meaningful* work (as defined by whoever is doing the work) - that is, the stripping away of unnecessary labour, to make space for desired life work.

## 3. Nurture the Nature
aka **Human Condition(ing)**
TM conceives that every human is born with a particular nature, and that it should be our duty and pleasure, as individuals and as a society, to nurture this nature, to pull the best out of it, provide the necessary conditions for potential to be realised/actualised, and equip/support each other to mitigate the worst of it - i.e., we should all strive to get the best out of each other, and help each other overcome our worst tendencies. Can view each human as a seed, which of course needs optimal conditions (sunlight, water, nutrients) to grow according to its potential, and which can be stifled when "raised" in the wrong conditions. So, it is of utmost importance, if we want a thriving, flourishing species (and planet full of species), to design our society in a way that fully nurtures this nature, tailored to each individual and their unique disposition & inclinations. And so, TB is interested in considering how to do this, and how mindscaping and streamlining the economy can promote this.

This all relates to the general **human condition**. TM likes the term "condition" because of associations with *medical* conditions and its potential to be applied to all conceivable (positive and negative) experiences associated with being human. So, "human condition" can be an umbrella term - a container - for all these conditions and experiences. A *generaliser* or *unifier* or *commonliser*, maybe? A mechanism by which we can recognise our innate unity, commonalities in our experiences, find bonding ground. And then, the appending of "ing" to "condition" (transformation from noun to verb) emphasises that this is a process/activity, and hence, can be refined through continuous experimentation and observation. "Human conditioning" encapsulates and reminds of the fact that we should consciously strive to condition ourselves, as a species, to unleash the best of ourselves, and transcend the worst.

# Resource vs Area
Useful to contrast 2 approaches to learning/doing: (1) resource-first vs (2) area-first. Could replace "first" with "led".

Resource-first (R-led) is about identifying a resource that would be useful for learning a particular thing (e.g. (text)book, course) and following along (actively engaging) with it. For this to be most effective, requires good choice of resource (need to pre-assess whether the resource is worth allowing it to dictate your focus when learning the thing it's about, or whether it should only be "dipped into", consulted when needed).

Area-first (A-led) is about identifying a particular area/domain/discipline/concept you want to learn, and being led by this in e.g. finding resources to input to fill knowledge gaps within this area. This involves a more "patchwork" approach of consulting many different (re)sources around the same topic/object - includes e.g. synoptic(al)/syntopic(al) reading, and self-determined implementation/consolidation points (to implement learnings by e.g. doing exercises, writing summaries).

Could see it as:
- R-led is **integrating a knowledge mound** - identifying a set of views (**knowledge mound**) you want to become part of your worldview / total knowledge, and then integrating that mound.
- A-led is **filling a knowledge gap** - identifying an *absence* in a set of views (a shakiness, uncertainty, lack of understanding, **knowledge gap**) in your worldview, and then filling that gap.
