- I just made the discovery: if gcd(a, b) = 1, by Bezout's lemma, you can find integers n, m such that an + bm = 1. But then, this also implies gcd(m, n) = 1, gcd(a, m) = 1 and gcd(b, n) = 1. But this only works when the LHS = 1.
