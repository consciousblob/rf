- 	- I'm enjoying this new perspective on existence and consciousness. I've started the view things like this. We're all partaking in what is effectively a slow but sure, subtle, emerging alien takeover. All humans today (and since we first became what we'd consider to be "human") are incarnations of extraterrestrial consciousness. That's what allowed the development of the "kind" of consciousness we associate with being human, which is effectively a self-consciousness - a consciousness of our own consciousness. These bundles of extraterrestrial consciousness have different experiences, origins, levels of natural (ease of) integration and differentiation, based on how relatively "integrated" their origin node is/was, how long / how many lifetimes they've spent as self-consciousness, and how integrated the nodes in which they've experienced incarnations have been.
	- For example, a (self-)consciousness that has experienced many lifetimes in a highly integrated ("advanced") node (or nodes) will be relatively integrated by default. These are humans that generate the most novelty, feel most opposed to subordination, conformism, arbitrary norms, are in most desire of "more" and "better", want more for themselves and humanity, are more concerned with self-actualisation and reaching potential (and helping others do the same). The most non-conformist individuals who simultaneously desire what is best for all (so, they're not non-conformist for the sake of it, but because the norms we conform to are holding us back). These individuals are the *least likely* to say things like "this is just the way the world works" and accept sheer stupidity due to lack of imagination. They are least likely to oppose others' attempts to "think outside the box", imagine ideal alternatives, engage in idealistic thought, etc. They are the least likely to make fun of someone for being "weird" (because they are likely "weird" themselves due to coming from node(s) with vastly different - more effective, efficient, divergent - ways of operating, because in order to progress, to become "advanced", they recognised the need to embrace divergence and foster diversity in thought and expression of thought (and self)).
	- Indeed, the way I'm conceiving of and representing these existential/philosophical/spiritual ideas is to try to position myself as "one of the ones" whose consciousness has more experience in more integrated nodes (and originates from a highly integrated node), relative to most.
	- And you might, understandably, perceive this as incredibly arrogant - for me to think this of myself. But can you relate? Can you relate to feeling so utterly "strange" (and being told that consistently - both implicitly and explicitly - throughout your life from different people), to feeling like your thoughts are so vastly "alien" to most individuals around you (towards which you've collected evidence by observing reactions of confusion, weirded-out-ness, rejection, mis-or-non-understanding, after expressing only the smallest fraction of these thoughts)? Do you relate to having to hide most of your thoughts (or, as I'm doing now, attempting to express/broadcast them on a platform) to maintain social cohesion, to feel comfortable to exist around others, and not worry about negative judgement? Do you constantly have an un-switch-offable subconscious assessment of how others perceive everything you do (and everything everyone else does), according to your conception of their worldviews? Do you consistently, persistently create alternate realities, fantasies, worlds in your mind to escape to, to make basic daily living more tolerable, and not completely, utterly, exasperatingly, out-of-your-mind *boring*? Do you constantly contemplate how we should restructure society, reorganise ourselves as a species, in order to make more collective progress, actualisation, and pursue the freedom and flourishing of all? Can you just not stop thinking about the broader meaning of life, and find yourself unable to engage "productively" in any activity you deem as anything even slightly departed from the conception of meaning you've arrived at?
	- If no, then please allow me to reject your assessment of my arrogance. While you are welcome to it, I am welcome to reject it.
	- If yes, then you are probably also a "relatively alien consciousness", and have also experienced more lifetimes in more integrated nodes, relative to most. Yay! We have identified like-minds.
	- If you still perceive this framing as unconscionably arrogant and despicably superiority-signalling, allow me to self-justify. The whole point of "integration" and integrating is to see the inherent value in/of all, and to not elevate oneself *existentially* above any other entity; this is exactly what I do and intend to continue. The point of *differentiation*, on the other hand, is to identify the spheres/realms in which you excel and outperform others; this is exactly what this identification of/as relatively extra-terrestrial is and does; this is (one of) my way(s) of differentiating. Embedded within this worldview is a notion of both superiority and equality. You can sense feelings of superiority when we express any othering that differentiates us as possessing some marker/trait that could/would be perceived as superior by most - in this case, the self-perception of having more "existential experience", being, basically, an "older" consciousness. But in this, there is also a recognition of inherent equality of all, because we all originate from the same Source, and little blobs within the same big Blob of consciousness; would you consider a particular wave in the ocean "better" than another?
	- Anyway, one reason I'm leaning into this framing - framing myself as a relative extra-terrestrial - is
	- I'm now also rethinking age. There is human age, and consciousness age. We sometimes say a person is "wise beyond their years" (usually referring to the relatively young), or encounter an older person with an (unendearingly) childish outlook on life (manifest in e.g. biases, fallacies - basically, psychological underdevelopment and immaturity). So, we already have the sense that wisdom is not necessarily correlated with age. But what about extending our conception of age to include *possible age in the universe* - universal age, the age of our consciousness? This could help to explain apparent inconsistencies and disparities and unevenness in how wisdom correlates with age. We already have phrases like "an old soul". I think it may be wise to incorporate such framings into our conceptions/understandings of consciousness, of ourselves. Older souls (bundles of consciousness) are in general going to display more wisdom from a younger age. This is another way to identify relative extra-terrestrials: is their apparent level of wisdom -understanding of / insight into the world - particularly unusual for their age? Or just, particularly unusual, regardless of age?
	- Integration is about equalising, identifying similarity, commonality, shared value(s). Differentiating is about identifying and cultivating areas of superiority (and concomitantly, inferiority). We need both.
	- I've adopted a new framing, of being a "relative extra-terrestrial". We are all terrestrial, by default. But we are also extraterrestrial (our origins extend beyond Earth, literally in terms of origin of matter, but also, in my worldview, in terms of origin of our self-consciousness). Our "soul" originated in a node that is not Earth - extra to Earth. However, some of us could be considered "more" extra-terrestrial, relative to others, in that we've had *extra* extra-terrestrial experience - that is, experience in relatively more integrated/advanced nodes.

I want to talk of the idea of being a **relative extra-terrestrial**, because I find this to be a deeply resonant concept (as in, it seems to amplify my internal energies, which I can channel into materialisation).

The reality (or, my reality) is that I find most things done and discussed in daily life - well, my current daily life - to be insufficient. Insufficient for what? I'm not exactly sure - how to explain. It's just a sense that they don't really scratch the surface of the things I perceive existence to hold, contain - the capacities to explore. The depth. To take a rather obvious analogy, ocean: it's like there's a bunch of stuff, certain species, that tend to spend most time near the surface, and there are different kinds at each "level" or "layer" of the water, going down to the very furthest depths - the seabed. A lot of unknowns. And that's where I want to go. Please forgive any ego exuding through this analogy and my placement of myself (or, my preference) at the "deepest" of the ocean. I just don't know how else to try to convey what I want, and what I feel is missing. What I crave. My deepest desires. To think in the realms not or never before thought within. To roam these realms, to carve my own paths to and through them, and beyond, inventing realms of my own. I just want them to exist. I want to discover, uncover what exists, what could exist, push the limits.

And so, I want to create a place for myself, in my mind, where I feel that this is "valid". I am creating a framing of life/existence in order to rationalise any thoughts and activities I may entertain/participate in that others may consider as "strange" or *invalid* in some way. I want to shield myself and my mind from these negative perceptions, to maintain my mindal momentum so I can continue to travel into the "realms" I just harped on about. And this is currently my preferred way/means of doing this.

I choose to view myself as a "relative extra-terrestrial". This contains multiple meanings, reasons for being:
- Relative to other terrestrial beings (incarnated on Earth), I may generally be perceived as quite "extra", which is my preferred translation for others' use of "strange", "weird", etc.
- I want to "add stuff" (extra stuff) to the terrestrial experience. I want to be extra-terrestrial.
- I believe I've had many lifetimes as an extra-terrestrial in nodes with vastly different modes of operating, higher level of integration to humanity's current - basically, my origin node diverges significantly from this (terrestrial) node. And so, it doesn't really feel like home here unless I allow myself to entertain the realities that *do* feel more like home. In a way, I feel a desire to recreate my home (origin node) here on Earth - both in the miniature (in my mind and its various creations, like writings, art, music) and in the wider world (how society functions, showing the home to other minds so it may eventually feel like home to them too).
