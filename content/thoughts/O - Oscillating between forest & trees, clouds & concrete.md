title: O - Oscillating between trees and forest
---
I find that spending too much time in trees or forest mode leaves me feeling a bit empty - mentally, emotionally, even spiritually, unfulfilled.

For example, if I can't see how a tree I'm examining in a lot of detail contributes to my comprehension of and connection with the broader forest, I find it difficult to remain engaged; my thoughts naturally drift to the "clouds" or the "bigger picture".

Likewise, if I spend too much time "thinking big" and playing around with what would probably be deemed by most pie-in-sky ideas, idealistic (perhaps to a fault), I feel a sense of dissatisfaction. I feel as though I don't have any tangible, "realistic", on-the-ground action to back up this "grand vision".
