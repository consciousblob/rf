title: O - hallucinated desire
---
When deprived of things necessary for survival (sleep, food, water, etc.), we tend to hallucinate.

When deprived of things necessary for thrival (meaning, purpose), we tend to hallucinate. We can hallucinate *desires*.

That cheap plastic toy you can buy in the gift shop, or from Temu. Who wants that? Is that a true, ultimate want for *anyone*? Probably not. And yet, we buy. Because we're so fucking deprived of meaning that we're turning to madness, and hallucinating what we want.

I don't want to claim that I know what a person wants more than they do themselves. But I will claim that any individual who mindlessly buys such items is likely not very in-touch with their own sense of purpose, and hence, their *true* wants.
