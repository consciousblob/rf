title: O - harnessing hard work of others to destroy hard work of others
---

Those who have accrued the power to harness the hard work of others, to set the current courses of action, scopes of work - they are effectively harnessing the hard work of others to destroy the hard work of others, across our evolutionary pathway. All the technological progress will be for none if we destroy ourselves and our environment (and ourselves, and environment, cyclically), drive ourselves to extinction. How dare we collectively harness each others' hard work to passively observe, or actively contribute to, the destruction of the hard work of others? What a ridiculous situation to find ourselves in.
