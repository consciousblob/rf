# Converge

- The fundamental unit of the ecosystem is a **thought**.
- Every thought relates to one or more objects.
	- Want to be able to extract objects (what things could this thought be said to relate to) at different levels of generality/specificity.
	- Want to see connections between objects, and link thoughts via objects.
	- Anyone could e.g. create a hub to represent a particular object of interest (e.g. climate change adaptation in the UK, climate change adaptation in general, climate change in general, etc.) and organise it how they feel works best / how they see fit. They can choose to publicise the hub so that others can either view or contribute.
- Hubs to collate hubs
	- You can even create a hub to organise/present other hubs in a coherent way that makes sense to you
- Each hub starts on an individual mindscape
	- Then, if the hub gains traction and other people find it useful, could be promoted to the general mindscape / thought space so you don't have to host it with your own hardware.
- The ecosystem can be thought of as a network in which each node is a **thought space**.
- Every mind has their own thought space, in which they provide (output) and receive (input) thoughts.
- You can customise your thought space by choosing various mindware - a pick-n-mix of modules that provide different features/functionalities, based on how you want others' thoughts to be presented to you, and how you want to present your thoughts to others visiting your thought space.
- A thought space (node) can also be a **hub**, which can represent a set of minds plus thoughts on how those minds relate to each other / what the hub is/does.
- Hubs allow minds to self-organise in ways that enable fluid, flexible, easy communication, collaboration and co-development of thought (interthinking), which can invoke action - projects/pursuits/formation of organisations in the physical (beyond digital) world.
- Some examples of what hubs can be/represent:
	- A local/national/global community
	- A business/organisation
	- A government / governmental department
	- Academic journals / publishers
	- Researchers in a particular discipline
	- A project to develop a new mindware module (or literally anything)
	- A community of enthusiasts for a particular interest/hobby
	- Etc.

# Diverge

- Starting from school
	- From a young age, we're introduced to the mindscape, and given our own mindscape - space on the net to participate in society's thinking endeavours, to upload and download thought, enhance and benefit from collective intelligence. We are encouraged to share thoughts, and engage/interact with others' thoughts, cultivating our own digital garden. Gets us into the habit of contributing to society, being active participants, from young age. More effective learning, because we're also kind of teaching stuff by creating explainers. Also cultivating creativity, expression & autonomy because we're experimenting with how to uniquely represent our thoughts.
	- We can also participate in the development of mindware, learning programming and open-source development at youth.
	- As we go through school and general life, we record thoughts. We submit/upload work. Complete projects. Append to and develop the thoughts of others. Identify hubs of interest and look our for opportunities to participate in them. 
	- So, our mindscape forms a digital repository of all our contributions to society throughout life - a portfolio of work, interests, thoughts, creations, collaborations. And this can be used to help identify best directions to pursue for "career" and how our unique inclinations, dispositions, interests, skills, experience can best contribute to society. It can help to match us with others keen to work on similar things, and hubs representing different types of work in society. We can have mindware in form of e.g. AI system that helps with this.
- **Portal into society**
	- Our mindscape is like a portal into society's digital exiistence, and by proxy, physical existence. It is a way for us to engage with the world, with various communities, to cultivate a global existence/presence.
	- The mindscape mediates our interactions with the web, and with each other via the web.
- Curate knowledge collections
	- We all get into the habit of and normalise sharing tidbits of learning, knowledge - e.g. explaining an example or problem, a situation, giving an overview of a topic, discussing a phenomenon, etc. Each output could be considered a thought.
	- Then, you can search for thoughts pertaining to a particular object (e.g. Lorentz transformations of electromagnetic field tensor, links between information theory and the fediverse, or just information theory, just electromagnetism, just fediverse). 
- Should be fundamental, basic right to have a personal thought space, and connect to net & global thought space / Mind.
- "mind" includes biological and artificial
	- Fundamentally, whether human, non-human animal, LLM, or some other AI system, everything that could possibly constitute a "mind" is just a (unique) processor.
	- Thoughts are both the produce (input) and products (output) of these processors.
- Thought capture
	- Fundamentally, the purpose of mindscaping is to capture and manipulate thought - to become thoughtsmiths - craftsmen of thought.
	- First, we need to capture the fundamental substance - thoughts themselves. Then, we need tools to shape this substance into different interesting and/or meaningful/useful things, objects.
	- Once we have efficient ways of capturing thought, we can think of best ways to integrate it and then differentiate from these integrations.
- E.g. a hub for anything (a book, an idea/concept, movement, philosophy, organisation, product, film, etc.), which collates all notes from all minds related to that thing (e.g. where the note explicitly tags the/the hub representing it)
	- Then, you can collate the "top thoughts" (with the most upvotes) related to this thing, set a cutoff (e.g. top 1000 thoughts) and have an AI tool synthesise them and pull out key things and/or things directly related to what it is you want to extract from/about that thing
	- So, querying others' thoughts on anything / any object of interest
- Generalisers (convergers, integrators) & specifiers (divergers, differentiators)
	- A certain system(/software/module) (e.g. LLM) or set of systems is dedicated to extracting commonalities from all thought.

- You have a thought:
	- *Wouldn't it be great if we could self-organise in such a way that we all divert our working efforts toward producing/providing only what we collectively agree is essential, and then have loads of free time (because the essential work is divided between way more individuals), and then with this time, those with an inclination to nurture the young can form groups where, for example, you offer to teach maths to a group of 6 of a certain ability (independent of age, where ability is gauged by parents based on whether the kids can answer a few sample questions without difficulty), and then there is a set of individuals in the community offering various teaching sessions, and a community session bulletin which parents can sign their kids up for sessions, and you teach them for a "season" and then they rotate to some other group or something like that?*
- Now, this thought is obviously quite chaotic and nebulous and touches on several different areas, and hence could probably be atomised into several distinct but related thoughts. It's underdeveloped, emergent. It's a seedling - the beginnings of a more developed idea for an ideal - something that, in an ideal world, would exist. It needs to be planted, and nurtured, grown, developed.
- But ultimately, you're interested in finding answers to Qs such as:
	- *Have other minds considered something like this (if so, which)? Does anything like this already exist? What are others thinking about related to this? What thoughts already exist that overlap with this? What more established areas/fields/disciplines exist that discuss things like this, and where can I find related outputs (e.g. academic literature, videos, books)?*
- So, it would be cool to have a way to just e.g. input this thought into an artificial mind (i.e., an AI system) which then searches/synthesises all thoughts it identifies as relevant to the area (based on conditions/restrictions/filters you specify, to reduce the search space) and provides you with an overview of what has been thought about related to your thought with links to specific most relevant thoughts on other nodes.
- The point of this all-over-the-place slightly specific but also slightly general thought example is to illustrate the kind of situation we might often find ourselves in - this is the case of having an un(der)developed thought where you might have some of the details figured out, but the rest is very nebulous with no clear form, and you only have a gist understanding of what exactly you mean, and yet, you want to get an idea of / feel for what thoughts already exist that are similar to this nebulous thought.
- Currently, the closest thing to this is via LLMs, which provide a very high-level overview/synthesis of existing thoughts - but, the limitation is the lack of specificity in originating minds, and it's this specificity which would allow like-minds (in particular areas) to convene, collaborate and deepen/develop their thinking and materialise it into something useful.
