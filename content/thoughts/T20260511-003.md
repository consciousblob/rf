title: Don't share with people who don't understand
---

This thought is born out of a frustration I've experienced with my mum. This has always been the case: whenever I start talking about something I'm passionate about, like mindware, she cuts me off and says she isn't interested. So for a while, I stopped saying anything about it. Then, today, she had an "idea" that had a lot of overlap with mindware, so I got excited and mentioned that I've been working on mindware again, and she seemed willing to listen. Then, after a few minutes of me explaining my current ideas, she said "alright, that's enough - you've given me enough information." And it was really hurtful, because this is one of my biggest passions in life now, and I think about it *a lot* and the fact she just isn't interested hurts my feelings.

But it made me realise - and I don't care how egoic this sounds, I feel I need to restore some faith in my own passions - some people are just not capable of understanding or envisioning how transformative or important something can be. Or, they just don't have the patience or mind to entertain these transformative ideas of trying to completely revolutionise something. So, don't waste your time trying to explain to them. Develop. Build. And what you build will speak for itself.
