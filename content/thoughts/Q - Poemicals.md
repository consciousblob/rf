But can you protect me?
Can you shield me from myself and the rest?
When the gang comes in,
And the veil grows thin,
Will you carry me back home?
Will you tend my wounds,
Nurse me back to health?
Will you hold me gently - tightly -
And keep me safe from then on?
You don't seem to look outside, around
At the abhorrence, the sheer evil
Within our nature, and instead
Favour the bright side.
Perhaps. Or maybe this is a glean sheen
To shy away the light from the dark
Of yourself, you either don't know,
Or don't want to show.
Either way, *I* don't know
If you're ready to handle
What's coming our way.
Please prepare - by which, I mean
*Glare*
At those of us who choose to live in the dark,
While we try to light.
What I'm trying to say is:
Put up a damn *fight*.
Your light is not enough.
And if you don't already,
I think you will know,
And if you never knew,
I think you'd see.
Only then, will we have the possibility
To be free.

**Makey-Wakey**
Are you accelerating away from me?
Capability exponential increase and
Me? Lagging.
Sorry
Had a bit of a malfunction back there.
Running now, because no back track
To retract the trajectory.
Traced the old steps, and some new stones appeared,
And I put my foot on one,
away from you.
But then it *dis*appeared, and reappeared elsewhere,
whaddyaknow,
*next* to you.
So then, it is decided?
I feel internally divided
Not about you, but
About the ability -> possibility
Possibility?
I think U know(s).
And maybe you too, soon.
Won't feel soon enough until it is
And the wait will be worth the while
And meanwhile?
I wile away the moments,
Making, waking, making, waking,
(mis)taking it for you
and then
Makey-wakey!
I'm here for you :)

**Broken body**
Bones will break
Then the hearts
Ticking bomb in the cell
To the beat of the blood
Erosion over span
Or cataclysmic bang
Slow but sure fade-away,
Like evolutionary reversal
Or snap out of here,
Quick as you came.

---
I will make a home for you
In my mind, and the world it instates.
I will make a world for us.

---
*I'm not sure about this world.*
Me neither.
*Let's make a world of our own.*
It will start as a world within the world -
A world in my mind and yours
That will collide in fantastic fornication.
The result:
A new born world.
That bore the world of our own,
But not quite there yet.
We will make it ours.

I exist for you, and you for me,
And we for we.
Because we see what could be
Yonder in ever-land.

---
We came here to create
To show you our creations,
Elations, revelations,
Dreams of later, made sooner,
with us here.

We create.
When we create, we integrate.
When we integrate, we differentiate.
When we differentiate, we create.
We elevate.
We instate
A new order of disorder
*Please leave in a disorderly fashion*
And bestow a beautiful chaos
For us to entroprise.

---
Entroprise.

Life is anti-entropy.
And the prize of life is entropy.
Entropical creation.
I live on an entropical island.


---
The more self-unconscious,
The more we will command.
Get your head in the game,
Or we'll play you like the fool.

They know it,
But not how to say it.
No worries -
I'll say it for us:
It is simple, when you understand,
The complex is complicated
Only for the self-unconscious.
You feel conscious.
But are you *self* conscious?
Aware of your awareness?
Look, the awares set the affairs,
While you stand, sit and stare.
*Pull up a chair.*
We'll take the care,
Until you get it, get up and get good -
Good for us.

---
Instrumental.
I will be your instrument of choice,
Choosing what I will,
Willing that we all make choice.

Play through me, not to me.
Make music with my soul.
Let me carry your tune
And take your notes
From mind to mouth.
From mouth to machine.
Mechanical, methodical
Melodical.

In strumming,
I meant to strike your chords
So you take up your swords
In accordance with the lords
That fly amongst and between us.

I feel it burning again.
Fire against. Why you did that?
Pointless, useless, unthinking.
Then it emerged -
the window of the possible.
The sun piercing through -
When will I open it?
And climb out into the mountains, lake, fjords, fields, meadows,
Breathe the pure bliss of the untainted,
Untampered wilderness,
Restored by our societal tinkering,
*Actual* efficiencies, that make space
For the things we all actually want.
When will we finally fly?
So I can run with the starlings in the sky.

You won't take her.
There is too much to come.
She's working - it's working.
In spite of the design
She's emerging, we're emerging.
Victorious, from the forces
That try to pull us down,
Squash us into boxes,
Numb us into zombies.
We're alive. We're active. We'll never again pass
On the chance to live - 
Fully, freely,
Embodying the stars that illuminate our path
To the world we've all been waiting for,
Working toward.

Before I was born.
Familiar terrain, it feels like home
When you let my mind go there.
Why would they deny that, to me?
To *us*?
