E-merge.
We merge into a blob. Then, we emerge from this blob into our own blobs. Then we merge these new blobs into another new blob. Then we emerge as new blobs from the newly merged blob.
Ad infinitum.
This is the existential merging process. This is (e)mergence.
And until we realise this, we remain in the existential emergency.
