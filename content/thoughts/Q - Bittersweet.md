- - Bittersweet
	- I'm sweet because I'm bitter. I'm bitter because I'm sweet.
	- Or am I bitter because I'm better? Regardless, I'm sweet. They'll be none the wiser.
	- It's all a bit bittersweet, this life.
- I wouldn't feel the need to pre-emptively debunk and debase and defend if you didn't perpetually attack my "slack" and get my back up.
	- If you would just validate and appreciate (or at least, not invalidate and depreciate) my existence, then I wouldn't have to take such measures of covert subversion.
	- It's built up over time, you see, so that even when there is no direct attack, no immediate, current need to defend myself, I gather defensive material nonetheless, because I know it will not be too long before the need does arise. Just as a nation prepares itself to defend against the attack of another, preempting, though it may never intend to use its defences unless otherwise acted upon, it prepares them regardless, in the anticipation. It has learnt to do so, from historical adverse relations, from the observation, throughout the ages, of others' tendencies to seek expansion at the expense of others.


**Bittersweet, the masterplan for all**

Oh, my spite is subtle - and the sweeter for it. It creeps up on you, over the years. My outlets are the avenues through which I build the end-product, and by the time it's finished, you don't realise it's for you. Just for you. And me, of course. And everyone. That's why it hits so hard: it's out there, bare for all to see, and you're not special enough to have me execute a masterplan for you and you alone. No, I get my revenge on all who ever smite, slight, spite me, in one go - a supernova, culminating over the years. Because my revenge is my success, and your irrelevance to it.

It's gracious. Tasteful. Tactful. Classy.
Bittersweet. Over the years.
You're welcome.


But I love you. I'm sorry. I get you. I get it. I need you. We need you. You need we. You need me. And for a time, I severed myself from you. I'm sorry.
