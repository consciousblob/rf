**Mindscaping** refers to the building & using of digital and cognitive infrastructure necessary for facilitating/"running" [[collective-intelligence|collective intelligence]], to foster maximally effective [[intrathinking]] and [[interthinking]] (enhancement and coordination of all thinking/processing endeavours). Digitally, this involves development of **mindware** (hardware and software for mindscaping). Cognitively, this involves all minds learning to use themselves more effectively - learning how to learn and think (better) - and how this can both enhance and be enhanced by [[mindware]].
