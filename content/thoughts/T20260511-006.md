I'm just going to brainstorm some ideas for what I want the site / my general array of outputs to include:
- Better
	- Problems/questions (P/Q) and potential answers/solutions (general ideas on how to address)
	- Each P/Q comes under a particular area if relevant (e.g. better food, better healthcare)
	- Each P/Q has associated thoughts, displayed alongside it
	- Each "better X" area has associated thoughts, displayed with it (thought which are not associated w/ a particular P/Q)
	- A particularly special space for e.g. "better society" or "better world" (which encompasses all the other "better X") that fundamentally, explores ideas for different ways of organising society and structuring our economy to promote progress and thrival (beyond survival)
- Possibilities, imaginings
	- Exploration of possibilities via discussion, ideation, fiction/stories, images, art
- Living investigations/explorations/scopes
	- Two types: broad areas, and singular questions
	- I guess this overlaps with the Better P/Q above, but basically, I want to be able to launch an exploration/investigation, and then continuously update this
	- It will obvs have "last updated" date
	- Examples
		- **Where are we at with genomics?** Investigate current status of genomics research, latest developments, core unresolved P/Q, limiting factors, current foci
		- **What is the current status of AI?** This would be very extensive, updated very regularly, due to fast pace of evolution. But would include considerations like:
			- How are most people using AI? What are the most popular AI tools? How are the top 1% (most knowledgeable power users) using AI - i.e., creative, novel ways most haven't considered? What do leading experts project for future based on current developments (current opinion scoping)? Which are the most "powerful" models right now (based on various quantitative and qualitative criteria I define)?
			- Could e.g. do a continuously updated survey - people continuously describe how they're using AI (in their own digital garden), and if they sign up for the continuous survey, an AI system scrapes what they share about AI usage, and then collates and presents this as a synthesis of how people are currently using AI. Updated in real-time.
			- Could repeat this methodology for basically anything (like "what problems are you currently experiencing in life?", "what is your experience of healthcare/transport/education system?")
			- Could also ask e.g. what are your top concerns in AI/questions you want answered? And then the particular agent/system responsible for this scoping would collate the most common questions, and base its investigations on these.
	- what should i call these investigation/exploration objects? I would like a term that unifies both exploration and investigation (and/or scoping) - like IE, but better.
		- maybe something that plays with scope/scape/scrape. Like, scoping the area, digiscaping, mindscaping, infoscaping, datascaping? Scraping the digiscape to scope a particular space/domain.
	- This could also include more one-off investigations/explorations for more specific questions that I might not update (or, update that often)
		- Does the chemical treatment of water have any negative consequences?
		- How did the current global economy emerge? Overview of history of economic development
		- A comprehensive visualisation of all coups throughout history based on how many people were directly impacted by them
- My Worldview
	- An overview of my current understanding of everything, life philosophy, etc.
- Incubator
	- Ideas that I want anyone to take and run with
	- Provide mindware that others can use to create an idea hub/collective so we can link our ideas together (a system for matching ideas with executors/implementers)
	- A specific section for mindware/collective intelligence, as most of my ideas will probably pertain to this
- Artifacts
	- My version of "articles" which can be short or long -form, and present some thoughts/views on something. One type of article is a walkthrough of the development of a particular mindware functionality. Another might be a deeper-dive into a particular object in my worldview (like collective intelligence)
	- also includes much older outputs - raw version, and updated/edited
- Perspective simulator
	- (1) Real human, and (2) AI-generated
	- (1) Using the "continuous survey" model described above, we can ask e.g. "how's life for you at the minute? What are the key things on your mind? What's happening in your life at the minute? What are you worried/excited about? What gives you a sense of meaning?"
	- Could have these as specific questions that people answer directly and/or prompts that people can write a general response to (and e.g. LLM extracts specific answers to individual questions)
	- Then, AI system synthesises these - maybe on your personal, private digital feed, it randomly selects 3 perspectives/passages (written by real humans, anonymised if they wish) for you to get a glimpse of others' perspectives. The AI also presents analytics of e.g. common themes, concerns, thoughts, feelings, ideas, etc.
	- Also would be good to e.g. show literal images/videos from people's lives, locations, different countries, cultures, etc.
	- (2) based on what it's learning about general human experience, AI can generate different perspectives that seem like a human reality, but are novel (don't actually exist), and do this based on e.g. biases of a mind. So, a mind can opt-into this particular worldview-enhancing mindware module, and this module learns about their biases (e.g. from things they haven't been exposed to, different "walks of life") and then chooses content tailored to expose them to these things (fill gaps in worldview) - like, what it's like to live in a certain type of poverty, or live in a place threatened by flooding from climate change, or live as a billionaire, etc.
- Reflexive reflector ? (think of good name)
	- Inspired by the above  perspective simulator, here, people are encouraged to journal/provide reflections, general thoughts, dump whatever's on their mind, as regularly as they wish, in their own personal digital garden/space. And then, they can choose whether to make that reflection public - 3 levels: (1) non-anonymised, raw reflection, (2) anonymised, raw reflection (3) anonymised, processed (where an LLM extracts core themes, but the raw reflection is not available for public viewing). Or, they can keep it private. You choose this for every reflection (perhaps opt-in, so they're private by default, but you could e.g. switch to opt-out so that by default, your reflections are public).
	- These reflections just represent general thoughts - a product/reflection of minds' current worldview.
	- Then, any reflections made public can be e.g. churned into an overall "zeitgeist"/thing representing current thoughts of humanity - extract so many valuable insights from this "fluffy" anecdotal data. Like, VERY comprehensive "sentiment analysis". Also, find convergence on ideas for things, and use this method to stress-test and flesh-out ideas - so, perhaps propose an idea, and everyone on the net(work) of digital spaces - this digital collective - expresses thoughts on it, and an AI system synthesises these, enabling individuals in the collective interested in implementing the idea to continuously refine their idea based on this ever-evolving pool of feedback/opinions/ideas on the idea.
- Root-finding
	- All of this is in the context of root-finding - my philosophy, worldview, methodology - basically, representing everything to me. Kind of like a very open(-minded) religion at this point. And so I would like to tie everything into this, always relate back to "roots" and finding them - diverging in order to converge on shared realities, shared ideals, answers to questions, solutions to problems, better ways of doing things, and fundamental truths.
	- So, I would like space on the site to thoroughly explain and visualise the RF philosophy, methodology.
