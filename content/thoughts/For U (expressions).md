title: For U (expressions)
---

Am I beautiful enough for u
In body, mind and soul?
Am I pure enough for you,
If you are as you have indicated?
Or purer,
For displaying otherwise?


I think I get you.
But I don't. No.
I want to give the space to surprise
Take aback -
Not everything I thought
In good and bad
And that's okay.
I think you accept me,
As is.
And, not quid pro quo,
but just because it's so
I accept you.
Whoever, however
You are my one.
And I want to be your one.
But I want the One
Of a universe that sees itself.

Needless division, subtracted
Then we add love
And multiply
To infinity and
Be.
	Yonder.
