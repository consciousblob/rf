title: "Essentialised economy - initial sketch"

I just made a recording (19) about the "essentialised economy" idea I was into ages ago just before psychosis and realised how important I think it is and that I want to make it a primary focus. I want to think about the best ways to communicate the idea and start implementing it. For example, starting with something "simple" like the community kitchen idea (it seems simple but I'm sure it's a lot harder to implement than it seems) and so anyone involved in the provision of this community food hub is automatically entitled to receipt of whatever it provides, within bounds agreed by the community. Then, ideally expand into other areas such as transport where e.g. anyone working for the national(ised) railway/bus companies are entitled to free journeys. Then, combine so that e.g. the food and transport provision become one network, so e.g. someone cooking in the community kitchen (a provider of essentials) is entitled to free public transport and a train engineer is entitled to free food from their community kitchen.
