title: S - Reading a good novel & humbling
---

I finished reading A Good Girl's Guide to Murder yesterday, and I found it to be an excellent novel. In fact, reading it was an incredibly humbling experience for me. I binge-read it in a day - I literally couldn't put it down, so it was a literal page-turner. It made me realise the immense talent and skill that goes into writing a good book, and the fact that there exist *so many* individuals capable of this, writing books on a regular basis, and I just feel like that would be such an enormous mountain for me to climb. It's others' abiulity to *complete* things I think I find so incredibly inspiring and humbling, in additoion to their talent, because I often lack follow-through. It's something I really feel i need to build self-trust with; I need to demonstrate to myself that I am capable of followthrough and completion. And indeed, I do wish to write a book. I see the value in books. But at the same time, I don't want to cordon off my ideas for an indefinite amount of time while I complete the book - particularly for ideas that I feel are important for *now*, like, related to how we organise ourselves as a species. But, I guess for my more "esoterical" philosophical and fictional ideas, a book format would be suitable. I kind of like the idea of rethinking the book model/format entirely and going with a "publish and edit as you go" type thing where you can see the evolution of ideas, archive ideas you no longer want to use, etc., and also, you have a timestamp too indicate when and where ideas originated, plus you can get instant feedback on whatever you're writing. And then maybe there is no end, like with a novel, but a continuous sequence of entries, stories, updates, etc.
