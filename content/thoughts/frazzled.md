I look around,
And I’m dazzled
By the world -
By the beauty,
The sunkissed everything
On this beautiful day;
The chirping birds
The happiness glow,
On the outside,
Is reflected within me.
A river of thought
Flows through my mind,
Guided by wonder, awe
And reverence of all.

Though this beauty,
Is not so blatant everywhere…
Is it?

Must it be found,
In the ditches of despair,
Caverns of gloom
And silos of suffering,
That lurk in all corners
Of a world that, to me,
Appears so imperfectly perfect,
So beautiful?

Or is this beauty only available
To the lucky ones,
One of whom is me?

Or perhaps,
Beauty truly is,
“In the eye of the beholder”
And my eye has been trained,
From birth,
To see it in everything
Due to the beauty
That surrounded me,
As my perspective formed.

I’m frazzled,
Just as I’m dazzled.
Because as I contemplate
This dazzling sensation,
Others have no luxury of
Dazzlement.
They experience only
Frazzlement.

So can I truly know what it means
To be frazzled?
Should I stick to my dazzled privilege?
Or reject it,
And become only…
Frazzled?

No.
I shall remain both frazzled,
And dazzled,
So that I have the tools
To help dazzle and de-frazzle
The world.
