## Extra
Part of my worldview is the idea of being a "relative extra-terrestrial", and a lot of my beliefs, thoughts, interests, conceptions - stem from this thought. So, I'd like a place to store them, separate to other "more convergent" ideas, so that these other convergent thoughts have the potential to be more resonant (or, taken more seriously) by other minds, as I can understand that this "extra-terrestrial belief" is not widely shared (and so, could distract/detract from thoughts a mind may find otherwise relevant, if I were to include this "extra" stuff).

So, it is "extra" as in extraneous - not strictly necessary to make sense of the rest of my worldview and its outputs. Also, "extra" in reference to the idea of relative extra-terrestrialism it represents.

## Better
A core part of [[my-worldview|My Worldview]] and life philosophy is the idea of "better", and centring all thought/action around this. So, there is a [[Public/Pages/better|Better]] page to hold thoughts for better things, for a better world. Many thoughts can be categorised by what aspect of the world they relate to - for example:
- Thoughts on e.g. improving agriculture and nutritional value of food could fall into a "Better Food" category.
- Thoughts on e.g. developing our renewable energy grid could fall into a "Better Energy" or "Better Infrastructure" category.
- Thoughts on e.g. car hiring hubs to supplement and potentially/partially replace car ownership could fall into a "Better Transport" category.
- Thoughts on e.g. effectively incorporating AI into teaching methods could fall into a "Better Education" category.
- Thoughts on e.g. exploring different economic structures to allow people to work according to projects instead of organisations/companies, could fall into a "Better Work" category.
- Etc.

There will be major overlap between many of these categories due to "the world" being a highly complex, interconnected system which is difficult to delineate into distinct subsystems like "Transport", "Education", "Work", etc. That is, there are often thoughts that will span multiple or all conceivable subsystems. For example, consider a thought/idea that proposes leaning toward an "apprenticeship approach" to education, which could e.g. partner with public transport organisations, and provide a direct school-to-work pipeline for individuals who would be interested in e.g. engineering or planning our transport infrastructure; in this case, the thought spans transport, infrastructure, education, work, and probably other areas. So, any categories assigned to thoughts should be taken in the context of this possible overlap, and not as an exhaustive/comprehensive classification of the thought by all possible relevant areas. The purpose of assigning categories is only to flag where I perceive thoughts to be directly related to each other, to highlight any "thought clusters" which could be collapsed into singular proposals/projects.

Basically, all thoughts on the Better page can be labelled as "Better X", where X is literally anything a thought pertains to improving [^improving]. However, some thoughts may be so difficult to categorise (or belong to so many categories) that I won't bother categorising them. In this case, the thoughts can be considered as belonging to the category "Better World", which is a parent category for all other "Better X" categories.

Ideally, we'd all have our own digital spaces in which we can output thoughts and tag them with these same/similar categories - "Better X", or even just "X" (not the formerly-Twitter platform - the thing the thought is about) - and then we can have various [[mindware]] modules/mechanisms that collate and/or synthesise these thoughts, so we can develop and converge on shared thoughts around how to improve things. And so, the [[Public/Pages/better|Better]] page (and this entire site) is my contribution to this ideal (hopefully eventual) digital ecosystem.

[^improving]: Often, we have thoughts not about how to improve X, but what is currently *wrong with* or *bad about* X. I think these thoughts also belong in the "Better X" category, because they provide anti-direction (on what we *don't* want), which implicitly provides direction towards what we *do* want. And so, all thoughts about the world (including pure grievances) can be considered as regarding "better" or improvement, even if only implicitly. However, on this site's [[Public/Pages/better|Better]] page, I'll do my best to make explicit the connection between the thoughts and how they could make things better.
