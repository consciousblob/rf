I would like to trial writing an article explaining something I'm doing whilst doing the thing. For example, if I'm developing a particular feature of my mindware, or a particular application using LLMs, or doing a mini research overview / deep dive, I can start writing an article alongside it, explaining my process, almost acting like a log for e.g. barriers and breakthroughs, realisations, pivots, etc., and the end product will be an organic artifact of my thinking while making/learning/doing that thing.
