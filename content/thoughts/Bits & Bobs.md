#### Spiritual antenna
Spirituality is a way of connecting with a greater meaning to life, if there is one. It’s an antenna we all possess. Some people’s are large and highly developed and sophisticated, and others’ are smaller; neglected. This antenna allows us to tune into the channel of greater meaning. And though there may be nothing on the end of the line (if there is no higher orchestration going on), I still want to keep the channel open. #### Purpose of life
Littered all over the self-help/development world are videos and articles about something to do with how to find your life’s purpose or stop trying to find your purpose. But I often wonder, what is purpose? What exactly are people referring to when they talk about purpose? And if we don’t define what purpose actually means, we aren’t adequately equipped to search for it. So let’s start by defining it.

I think everyone has their own unique idea of what a purpose is, but generally they could fall into two categories. We could be referring to a very specific cause or bundle of causes you want to dedicate your life to. Like ending period poverty, or providing peaceful end-of-life care, or tending to the health of coral reefs. A certain area you want to centre your career around, perhaps. 

Or we could be referring to a more broad description, or through the lens of a certain approach. Like, 

“My life’s purpose is to embody compassion and approach everything and everyone with complete peace and love”

Or,

“My life’s purpose is to leave the world a little better and a little happier than it would have been if I had never existed”

There’s a difference between your personal life’s purpose, and the overall meaning of life. The meaning of life is a more general, objective reason or collection of reasons for living.

There are two question we tend to ask when it comes to purpose:
What is the purpose of life?
What is my purpose in life?

In this article, I’ll be addressing the first question. (the second is discussed in this article)
