title: O - Sharing Thoughts
---

- It's a project of building the infrastructure for intrathinking and interthinking - just thinking. It's how do I represent/express my thoughts, for both divergence and convergence?

I'm in quite a perculiar situation. I have quite a lot of stuff written, dispersed across the cloud and local storage. Some of it was written years ago, some more recently. Most probably before my psychotic episode. And so, I'm just thinking, how can I share all this in the most coherent way, while also respecting chronology?

I want to indicate when e.g. a document/article was written, particularly when it was before psychosis, but I also want to be able to edit it in order to... make it better? So, I want to preserve the raw old thought, in its original form (as a representation of a prior version of my worldview), but also be able to shape it according to my current worldview. And I currently have no idea as to what is the best way to go about this. So, as always, I will experiment. I'll release raw, untampered-with substance in my digital garden, and only release the edited/refined versions here (the remixes, where I've remixed with my worldview).
