title: Options paralysis; new approach of logging stuff in MW, SK, RF, maths
---
When confronted with many things you could and should be doing, how can you decide what exactly, specifically to focus your energies on in the next moment? There are so many very productive/meaningful things I could be doing, and so by just doing one of those things, I'm better off than if I didn't. But is there a *best* option? Because there are so many things I definitely, concretely want to make into realities - ideas I want to materialise/actualise - and these require dedicated, directed, consistent, concentrated, focussed efforts. But right now, these ideas are so scattered. I feel that without concrete goals to aim towards, and general timeframes/self-imposed deadlines, I flounder. It's easier to waste time when you're under the impression you have time to waste. So then, maybe I need to induce artificial pressure. And maybe I need to just select something, and run with it.

In terms of [[mindware]], something I want to trial is having a subfolder in the Logs folder for each area of my life I deem important enough to have a running log of (as in, the areas I want to build/maintain consistency in). Currently, this includes MW (mindware), RF (root-finding), M (maths) and SK (skills & knowledge). Then, each has an "action log" which will store my actions for the day in that area. Eventually, I could merge/synthesise these and ask LLM to analyse/summarise what has been achieved, what actions were taken, things learned, and I think this would be especially helpful for e.g. publishing work on the evolution of mindware, and for revisiting past learnings in the Maths and SK areas.

I'll also try to instantiate logs for each area for the next day and enter some form of direction/aims to help guide my efforts for these areas and ensure I make good progress and move the needle on each.
