I only tracked about 4 hours today - as in, I consciously timed and recorded specific activities spanning 4 hours. On a good day with no other commitments (like today), I could get this up to about 6 hours at least. What went wrong? Where did I fall apart? Well, I did make quite a lot of audio recordings, which isn't necessarily lost time. But, I didn't get around to SK (upskilling and upknowing) and learning Tamil. I want to do better tomorrow, but how? Record everything I want to get through somehow? I feel like when it's digital (like, on my spreadsheet), it's easier to decide not to follow through because it's so mutable - so easy to overwrite. So maybe I should try relying less on the spreadsheet to "tell me what to do", and more on a physical, handwritten, old-fashioned to-do list.
