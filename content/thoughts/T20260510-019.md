title: O - AI subverting reliance on parents/family
---
Currently, we are incredibly dependent on a miniscule subset of the population - primarily, our parents. And if you aren't born with great parents, you don't have a great lot in life. In other cultures, this is mitigated/avoided via the *community* raising children. But now, with that feeling a bit out of reach in current individualistic culture/norms/socialisation, but with the ever-normalising prevalence of AI, perhaps AI could be our culture's current way of mitigating this. eventually, transition back to community raising may be better (though, perhaps not, as even that is a small subset of possible good "raisers").

So, AI can be used to provide support, guidance, resources, nurturing, positive environment.
