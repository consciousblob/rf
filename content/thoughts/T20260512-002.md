title: Hub - provision of exams
---
Exams are a good way to test ability (to "certify"/verify someone's current knowledge/ability) but also good way to learn, and identify things you want to work on. So perhaps in different disciplines, hubs for that discipline (or a dedicated exam hub) can create exams for their discipline continuously and then intake different cohorts of individuals who want to obtain some kind of stamp/certification/qualification that they passed this exam, and their grade (the status/prestige of which depends on the status/prestige of the issuing hub) and hence can be considered to have a certain level of competence in the discipline. This can go on their digital record/portfolio of actions/work/accomplishments.

I thought this would be useful because since exams are so infrequent, stakes feel high, this can affect performance, and make them less accessible. Basically, this frequent availability of exams, across many disciplines, areas, topics, could provide incentive to rapidly develop expertise on an area.

Obviously, the qualification received will have a timestamp and so can be considered in need of updating, depending on the disipline and if you want to practice/work in that discipline/have reputation/authority/credibility in that area.
