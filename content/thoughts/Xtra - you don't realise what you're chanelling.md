title: Xtra - you don't realise what you're chanelling
---
You may not realise that when you create in flow state, you're integrated. This means you're tapped into collective consciousness & intelligence. This means you can download and upload thoughts/feelings. And when you're channelling the collective consciousness/intelligence, you're basically downloading thought (and uploading it to our materiality - what's directly visible/experiencable for other humans). Downloading is like inputting, but since you're a unique processor (unique to your physical self in this lifetime), you "put your own twist" on the downloaded thought matter/substance - you transform it into something unique to you. But still, ultimately, you're a vessel for universal communication, collective intelligence. So, your creations are simultaneously yours, and everything's (everyone's). They are a product of universal/collective thought, and your own individual processing of that thought - integrated consciousness, and your differentiated consciousness.

But the more you think your creations are solely a product of *your own individual intelligence* - that is, you perceive your intelligence as separate to collective intelligence - the less you will recognise this. The more you will assign very specific interpretations to your own creations; you diminish your ability to view your creations from different perspectives, to notice how they can mean/represent different things (because a lot of the things they were intended to represent, by U, are subconscious during materialisation). In reality, your creations have *many* possible interpretations, and these are equally valid (when made from a place of integration - disintegral interpretations are nonsense, but technically, no one is able to assess the level of nonsensicalness / determine whether it is as no one has monopoly on truth). You become merely a vessel for U. But when you recognise/realise that you are *co-creating* with U (not an "independent creator"), you become both the vessel *and* substance carried by it - you become both the vehicle and its driver.

This is why I feel happy to and comfortable with utilising the creations of others to represent different thoughts - I see them as manifestations/creations of U. I choose to see/ask: *what was U trying to say when delivering this thought to and through this individual?* I find the individual's own conception/interpretation of their creation interesting, but ultimately, don't assign it too much importance. I have my own integral interpretation. And I want others to embrace their own integral interpretations of thoughts - including mine.

A thought visited you for a reason. You are best placed to materialise it, channel it, represent it. But the same/similar thought may visit others - possibly many - and they will have their own unique materialisation, processing, resulting in unique output. This is why I say: take the thoughts! No need to attribute them to a singular mind - i.e., mine. And in return, don't expect/ask that I attribute thoughts too specific minds. I might do when I perceive it to be useful, to serve a purpose. But otherwise, I'll take the thought, process it, and output my own. I encourage you to do the same. Input my outputs; process them uniquely (you can't do otherwise, when you integrate); output the (unique) products of this processing.
