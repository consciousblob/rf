Another reason I realised it's best to not publish my thought streams (for now) is because when I trialled it, I felt compelled to continuously generate new thoughts to ensure it looked like I'd "thought enough" every day (so, a performative over productive approach to thought generation). Whereas now, without that "publicising pressure", I can focus on actually synthesising the thoughts, not just writing stuff for the sake of it.
