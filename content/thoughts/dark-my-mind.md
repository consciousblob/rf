---
Sometimes, I look inside my mind
And what I see amuses me:
Simple thoughts, little ideas
And the clarity, I find refreshing.
If only everything was this sweet always, within
My mind

Other times, I look inside my mind
And I see things that
I never knew were there:
Parasites lurking in the crevices of consciousness,
Awaiting acknowledgement.
They notice when I notice them,
And prey on my awareness,
Assaulting my thoughts,
And tearing my soul
And I want you to understand the pain, the agony,
The spiral of torment that ensues such thoughts.
But ultimately, only I can see
The brutal battle field, that is
My mind

The way out is simple, theoretically
Body is vessel
Vessel is life
And the idea haunts and taunts,
Forever embedded in the fragile depths of empty chaos that is
My mind
