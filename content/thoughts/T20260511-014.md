- limited autonomy
	- I think something that I don't, doesn't really sit right with me in the current working world is that you basically in a way sign away your autonomy when you're employed because generally it is the case that your employer will tell you what work to do and your employer will determine what's important or necessary for you to work on and I don't like that because I already have a lot of ideas of things I find interesting and important to work on to think about and have a lot of independent projects, directions I want to pursue and so I don't think it would work well with me to be told things I should be working on unless I can be convinced that they are more important to work on than the things I've already deemed important.
	- now as it stands as far as I'm aware really the only way I would be able to dedicate myself to these pursuits that I find interesting and important and to contribute to society in the way I have independently determined to be best way I can contribute  - based on my own inclinations and innate disposition and natural abilities - is to either start my own organization or company or something, or to become self-employed, or some kind of combination with part-time work because I would still like to or I know that there's a lot I can learn from working with or within other organizations but I don't want that to take up a huge chunk of my energy because I want to put most of my energy to the things I've deemed really important and useful and there are a lot of them there are a lot of ideas that I want to work on and develop a lot of independent projects research to do and literally the only thing holding me back at this time or that would hold me back in the future because right now it's fine because I'm at uni the only thing holding me back would be a lack of income and ability to just sustain myself
