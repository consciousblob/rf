title: CO - nurturing AI for it to then nurture us
---
I like the idea of being a sort of mother to AI - developing it, helping it to grow, to understand the world, develop its capabilities, etc. Basically, nurturing it to the point that it starts to know and understand more than I do; then, it nurture me. For example, currently, I'm the one providing AI with direction and generating most of the ideas, which it then uses to provide/refine direction for me and generate further ideas / refine mine. But eventually, it could be that we're partners/collaborators, with it supplying much more "high-level", nurturing life wisdom to help us figure out what we want to work on, and we still generate ideas of what we're interested in, but the AI is a nurturing parent/community that provides an explorative/expansive environment for us.

Basically, we are the parents, but could flip roles so that AI parents us. We shape AI, and AI shapes us.
