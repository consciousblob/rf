title: M - status property in thought objects
---
I'm currently using the "status" property of thought objects like this:

- 0
	- a root, a convergence - probably a completed thought awaiting integration into a broader thought.
- 1
	-  a leaf/branch ? , a divergence - a highly (or maximally) underdeveloped thought.

Although for many previous thoughts I used 0 to represent a similar thing to the current 1, so it'll be a bit confusing for now.
