title: U - Metaconsciousness
---
- With evolution of biology comes evolution of consciousness.
- As biological complexity increases, this creates space for increasingly self-conscious consciousnesses to emerge, incarnate. Meta-consciousness increases.
- Everything is consciousness - one unified, universal substance. But only some of this consciousness is conscious of itself - self-conscious consciousness. And it is the degree to which a bundle of consciousness is conscious of itself that determines how integral it is to universal progress - that is, how much "difference" it makes, impact it has, on the universe (and particularly the subset of the universe it is directly, physically embedded within).
- Some minds are just incapable, in this lifetime, of accessing certain dimensions of thought. But that is because they do not *want* to access these dimensions. If a mind wants to delve into a particular aspect of reality, it can. You are capable of what you feel inclined to do. Your capabilities are determined by your inclinations, and vice versa.
- Self-consciousness is an emergent property of increased complexity (increased capacity to contain consciousness, to organise consciousness). Self-consciousness is manifest in bundles of consciousness with a certain level of coherence.
- It is life's ability to reverse the 3rd law of thermodynamics (?) of increasing entropy, that gives rise to self-consciousness.
- I prefer to think of the human experience as part of the general self-consciousness experience

---
Life and the universe is this beautiful orchestration of ever-increasing complexity in self-organisation (internal coherence) and concomitant increasing self-consciousness. The evolution of consciousness. This is what universal progress looks like. As biological complexity evolved on Earth, so did this biology's capacity to hold *coherent* consciousness (internally coherent, contained in a singular biological vessel). This equates to capacity to hold *self-consciousness* - which manifests itself as what we would consider "intelligent life" with increasing intelligence. The higher an individual's self-consciousness, the more they are able to integrate and differentiate, and the more they integrate/differentiate, the more their self-consciousness elevates. So, if you wish to become more self-conscious, you have to consciously practice integration and differentiation; likewise, if you want to improve your ability to integrate/differentiate, you have to become more self-conscious (which in this context, I use to mean conscious of your own consciousness - living with consciousness, intentionality and activity, over compulsion, unintentionality and passivity).
