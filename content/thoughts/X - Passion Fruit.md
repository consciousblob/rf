Without passion, we are hollow. We are shells. Fallible. Fillable with the substances of others' passion.

Passion is a driver. A force, causing action. A remedy, antidote, for inaction. Passion begets energy, which begets passion, etc. Passion energises.

Compassion is a passion from inherent sentiments of companionship. It is a passion for company - the company of humanity, on this little congregation of matter in the middle of nowhere (or everywhere) we call the universe. It's our place in space. Compassion is a companion to passion. It absorbs and emits passion. Compassion is automatically passionate, whereas passion is not necessarily compassionate.

Dark passions exist. Passion is an excitation of the soul - a perturbation. And what perturbs, can also disturb. And these disturbances evoke a disturbed state of mind, which is then prone to disturbing others. From this, disruption of some form is inevitable.

Consider a passionfruit. This could be the fruit of our passions - the culmination of hard work, emanating from a baseline passion, or a passionate disposition. Passion can induce action - relentless action - a background fire, which inspiration stokes, and whose smoke can choke an inexperienced handler.

Do you not realise, that the world is in your hands? That everything is at your feet, if you merely stand up, and look down and around? There is no, "that will happen in the future". It is happening *now*. All this time, past, now, and before you. It's all before you. You stand here. Look back. Before now, is everything you needed, to be here, and there in future. Look forward. Before you, is everything you are, and everything that will come, from you here. You see? It's all before you.
