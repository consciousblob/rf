title: O - other-defined success
---

The irony is that when we pursue something because we perceive that others view it as "success", this version of "success" only exists because others see this as "success", ad infinitum. So, we perform because we want the approval of others who perform, who want the approval of others who perform, etc., so that ultimately, it's all a big, pointless bubble of performativity. And at some point (in your life), that bubble will burst. You'll be confronted with the reality that relative to the meaning of life you've now consciously realised, everything you've been working for is meaningless. This happens when we don't take the time to consciously contemplate the meaning of life - *our* meaning in life. Even if we can't arrive at an objective Meaning, we can at least clarify our subjective meanings, and then live according to these. Only when we collectively burst this bubble can we avert this situation for the majority (or entirety) of humans living in modern society.
