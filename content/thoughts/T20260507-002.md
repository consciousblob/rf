Something I keep thinking about is the fact that I need to have some form of income after I leave university, and preferably large, because I would like to work towards financial freedom (because I want the freedom to explore/do whatever I want without needing to sell my labour to live in desirable conditions). However, the problem with "full-time work" is that by the time I've finished the work, I have little energy left to focus on what I'm actually interested in. So, it's important/ideal for me to have work/jobs that are directly related to / relevant for the stuff I consider to be my "life's work". And currently, that "life's work" pertains to developing mindware, considering (and implementing) improvements to collective intelligence and helping to improve how we structure society and the economy so we can focus on things we find most meaningful in the form of e.g. trying to start local food collectives based on shared ownership of land/resources and shared receipt of any profits/dividends.
So ideally, I'd be able to full-time dedicate myself to projects that directly move the needle on these aims and that allow me to have a singular focus on these (as I tend to work best when all my mental energy is directed toward one goal/purpose, one project at a time, not overstretched). And for this reason, I'm using this time while I'm at uni to develop my ideas, upskill, work on the kind of projects I would want to do as a full-time career, so that I maximise the likelihood of being able to make this my full-time work after graduation.
