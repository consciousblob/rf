He thinks I’m defective.
Factory production line faltered,
One of the duds,
Misshapen,
*Isn’t the blueprint perfect? Every other item seems to fit -*
*Seems fit for purpose,*
*The purpose of not having a purpose,*
*Because in my eyes,*
*That’s lame. Oh, so lame.*
You think lame is tame - at least,
According to your version.
But I think the others are tame -
Too tame,
Broken in by life:
Ridden by everyone else,
In every direction,
Because they lost theirs.
They need a map…
But it was replaced by the blueprint -
The factory’s symbol,
Pinnacle -
Glorious, grand *pièce de résistance*,
Because it doesn’t resist.
They don’t resist.
Why? Please do.
*Oh, but they do,*
*In their own ways, I guess.*
Yeah, I guess. Because I don’t know -
No one knows.
*Exactly!*
*So chill.*
*It’s not the end,*
*Not the start,*
It’s just… the same.
*The same as it’s always been.*
*From the start,*
Till the end.
