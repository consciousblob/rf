# Essential Work
- Healthcare
	- Providing specialised knowledge (highly qualified doctors)
	- Caring, nursing
	- Managing medical equipment
	- Medical/pharmaceutical research
- Transport
	- Vehicle manufacturing
	- Vehicle maintenance
	- Logistics
- Energy

---
Some realms of provision will be more prominent/important for certain communities. For example, a community with a port will have more provisional space (require more providers) of work related to shipping and maritime logistics.

If, for example, a toothbrush factory is based within a particular community, since the production of toothbrushes would be considered essential, anyone working in this factory could be given membership.
