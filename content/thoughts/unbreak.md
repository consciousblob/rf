Inertia.
Stopped, it stopped and my brain stopped,
So my heart stopped for that
Moment of oh dear
I forgot I have Obligations
Name them
I looked at myself
Inertia.
I felt something - something beautiful,
Then something terrible
It made me stop.
*Stop getting in my way!*
But I want to get in my way -
Wants me to fail?
Oh, how frail I am!
Tail between my legs,
I reflect on the wasted time,
Wasted space - *I*
Am a waste of space.
Useless, but could be useful -
Does that make me useful?
Does the potential to be of use,
Make a thing useful,
Or is it only useful when
It is used?
I’m used to feeling usefully useless,
Because I know I have uses -
Oh, so many uses.
But I don’t make use of them -
Don’t use myself,
And so though I’m not useless,
I become useless,
Can only be useful if
I use myself -
But how?? How to make best
Use? Otherwise,
I’m so useless.
So clueless.
So nothing.
Nothing.
Fading.
Failing.
Dead.

Alive again?!?
Circularity.
I’ve been here before. Surely.
But I repeat.
How to break out? Break it,
Without breaking myself?
Because if I’m broken,
I can’t break -
Break out,
That is.
*Give yourself a break.*
No thanks, I’m good.
Because I’m bad. No breaks for 
Bad ones, and then good ones
Don’t take breaks,
By *nature*. I need to become
Breakless.
Unbroken…
Eternally unbroken.
But how many times must I break,
Before I unbreak?
