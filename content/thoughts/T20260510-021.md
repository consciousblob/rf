title: MW - donation/charity fund-pooling site
---
- Basically, a space for people who want to donate to collectively decide where to donate to (like Giving What We Can, but allows input from all donators, weighted by their reputation in the space/collective)
- Everyone has a profile
- People have a "reputation" (like karma points on LessWrong) based on what you post/do and what the community thinks of it
- This gives you more "voting power" which is used for e.g. determining allocation of pooled fund to various charities
- So, if you have a high rep and you like a particular charity in the selection, you can assign a certain % of funds to it, indicating how much % of the total pool you think should go to that organisation/project/mission. Then, this % is weighted by your rep
- The combined weighted percentages determine the collective's allocation of funds
