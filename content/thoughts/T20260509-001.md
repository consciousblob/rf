title: "Language shaping worldview, media of expression, my language roadmap"
---

I am interested in how language shapes how we see the world (Sapir-Wolff hypothesis?) and since I am only fluent in English, I worry that this potentially limits possibilities in my perceptions, which could be expanded if I were to learn more languages. This kind of applies to various [[media-of-expression]], such as art, music, programming, dance, etc., although since all of these are kind of "language-universal" in that it's basically possible to create anything in terms of music/art/etc. using any language. Whereas, language itself is more "structural" and embedded in how we think. It is literally the medium through which we think. But there are other non-language means of thinking, such as visualisations, diagrams, symbols. I guess then primarily language is the only(?) medium through which we *[[expression-communication|interthink]]*/communicate.
Anyway, related to spoken/written communication - that kind of language/medium of expression - I think it's valuable to learn as many languages as possible to expand the lens(es) through which we see the world. I've been learning French on Duolingo for a while (but nowhere near fluent), I learned Latin for a bit (and think this could be useful, even though it's an "extinct" language, as it is a "parent" to many modern languages). But primarily, I'm interested in learning Tamil and/or Sanskrit. I like that Tamil is the oldest (?) existing language, and my grandad is fluent (his family is originally from South India, so Tamil is part of my heritage), which is additional motivation to learn. But also, it just seems like such a rich language, with a HUGE alphabet, and so I think, if I'm gonna put in effort to learn a language, I may as well dive into the deep end and pursue one of the most difficult I could (which it probably is?) Also, for both Tamil and Sanskrit, I like the prospect of reading ancient Hindu(?) scriptures and literature from the source.
I've decided to start learning Tamil semi-seriously. Eventually, I would also like to learn Mandarin, Sanskrit, Arabic, Greek, German.
