**Spatial & temporal barriers to integration.**

We tend to encounter 2 limitations that interfere with our ability to see what actions would lead to happiness:

- Spatial
	- This refers to a focus on an individual or set of individuals - either positive, or negative.
- Temporal
	- This refers to a focus on a moment. This could be past, present or future.

If too much energy is invested in either dimension (e.g. reminiscing about or regretting the past, thinking about a person you like/dislike, thinking about everything only in relation to yourself, projecting into the future), you will disintegrate and hence not be able to perceive optimal next actions to take.

Spatial/temporal are perhaps not the most fitting terms, but TM likes the distinction.
