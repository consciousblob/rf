title: SM - Triggering flow, inviting inspiration
---

One thing I'm starting to understand to be helpful for sparking that state where I perceive the possibilities and feel compelled to create is to input. Research something, or have an input or 2 on the go that discuss topics of interest, providing insights, information, knowledge on that thing. I find this tends to increase sense of urgency - to find out more about what I don't know (to marginally decrease the sphere of what I don't know I don't know, and increase what I do know I don't know).
