- allocating funds
	- I often find that I end up with loads of random bits of packaging, of different material, and have no idea what to do with it. I feel bad just throwing it away. I want there to be some kind of transparent reuse/recycle/repurpose/repair initiative. Some other people have already suggested this on the Global Board of Ideas, Initiatives and Projects, and it's under the general ideational category of "RRR". I am going to allocate 0.1% of my private income to the funding pot that funds projects (and prizes for projects) for this.
- regular societal detox
	- Everyone has the right to take 10% (or some percentage determined by their canonical/main community) time "away from society". There are societal detox/reset/refresh/insulation hubs dotted around where you can retreat to, have meals provided, with various facilities, for you to focus on whatever it is you want to focus on (reading, writing, creating, learning, researching, building, working, exercising, sporting, or simply relaxing/detaching/meditating - yes, overlap, but just trying to represent possibilities from as many angles as possible).
	- You can choose to have synchronous/simultaneous breaks with others (e.g. friends/fam), or on your own.
	- You can choose how/when you take these breaks - e.g. one day per week, 1 week per month, etc., with adjustments to fit allotted percentage.
	- In this time, you are not answerable to anyone. You are permitted to completely disconnect.
	- Would be sensible to have pre-arrangements/agreements so e.g. can arrange cover for your essential work.
