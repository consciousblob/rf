Basically, you use the properties of determinants combined with complex numbers to prove the identity that if a number can be written as a product of two numbers that are the sum of 4 squares, it itself is a sum of 4 squares. Then combine the fundamental theorem of arithmetic (every integer can be written as product of primes) with the fact that any prime can be written as a sum of 4 squares:
- any positive integer can be written as a product of primes
- any prime can be written as a sum of 4 squares
- any positive integer can be written as a product of numbers that are a sum of 4 squares
- every product of a sum of 4 squares is itself a sum of 4 squares
- any positive integer can be written as a sum of 4 squares
