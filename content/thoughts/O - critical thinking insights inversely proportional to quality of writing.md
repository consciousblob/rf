title: O - critical thinking insights inversely proportional to quality of writing
---
Source: [[Summer 2026]]

Been reading the novel a lot. I want to develop my critical eye, so I can have a commentary - meta-thoughts on my thoughts as I read the novel - instead of just lapping it up as given, without subjecting it to my own scrutiny and assessment of what is good/could be improved, etc. But I find I tend to do this automatically when the quality of writing is relatively poor; when the writing is intelligent, such as with this novel, I find it harder to think critically - or, criticise - simply because there is less (obvious) stuff to criticise.
