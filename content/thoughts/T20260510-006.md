Record/document everything you learn/do/encounter to accumulate your "learning context" which encodes your progress - struggles, breakthroughs, etc. Then, this context can follow you whenever you want to learn/do something new - you select the area you want to learn/do something in to extract all context relevant to that, which can be summarised/condensed, and then inform an e.g. LLM system which can provide guidance on what to focus on next, based on e.g. whether you want to revisit certain previously learned things (implement spaced repetition), etc.
