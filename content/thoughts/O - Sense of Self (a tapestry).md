title: O - Sense of Self (a tapestry)
---
Imagine your sense of self to be a tapestry, woven throughout your life. There are some core pillars on which your SoS can be thought to stand, that represent particular prominence/significance in your self-conception. These can be represented as hooks on which the tapestry hangs. The more hooks, the more stable the sense of self (like redundancy in distributed computing ?). If you base your entire SoS on just one thing (e.g. appearance, intelligence), then as soon as you perceive this to dip or disappear (the hook falters, falls), the tapestry collapses/crumples to the ground.
