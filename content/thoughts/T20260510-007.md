title: Sparks of inferiority from observing individuals considered successful
---
I've lately I've been watching some videos on a YouTube channel called EO that platforms founders that are considered very successful a lot of them are young, either my age or even younger or generally around my age and they describe their approaches to life and for example talk about how early they wake up how they spend every waking minute working on certain things and it does make me feel like hmm maybe this is what I should be doing and if I'm not doing that I don't feel adequate and it does make me think maybe I do need to be a bit more hardcore with how I do things because the thing is I do have the drive, the determination, the passion because when I am working on something I'm really passionate and interested in I can work on it and I want to work on it every waking minute I just don't allow myself to because I guess because of other people's expectations and that they well I worry that they might think it's unhealthy and I will receive criticism from it and I don't want to deal with the criticism so I'd rather just you know, take breaks or lay off when if I were for example maybe on my own or living on my own operating on my own schedule then I might be a bit more "intense" about how and how much I work. This is a core thing I'm learning the need to navigate and to overcome - a challenge. It's how to relentlessly pursue what you're passionate about and your conception of success without it being off-putting. To people around you and without alienating you from others who you care about and without it sparking criticism and negative feeling towards you. You know, I was watching a YouTube video by Teal Swan the other day about how you need to accept a certain level of pain if you want a certain level of success. So you have to decide on a trade-off and part of that trade-off may be sacrificing the approval of others if you want to really fully all out go for what you most want.
