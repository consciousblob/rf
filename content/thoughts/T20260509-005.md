title: "Community kitchen - idea sketch"
---

A "community kitchen" where anyone who contributes to e.g. cooking/ingredients automatically receives free meals, X of which have free delivery (and the delivery people get free meals as well). This would go hand-in-hand with the "community allotments" idea of matching people with gardens but little free time / gardening expertise to people who e.g. want an allotment. So, collectively home-growing produce to use in community kitchen.
