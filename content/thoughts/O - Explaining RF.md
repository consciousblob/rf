- Two kinds of roots
	- Objective - trying to uncover/discover objective truths, fundamentals of reality
		- Primary goal: pursuit of truth
	- Subjective - trying to uncover/create subjective meaning (informed by our pursuits of objective truth) 
		- Primary goal: pursuit of fulfilment, thrival
	- Inform one another
		- Our pursuits of truth can inform how we pursue thrival (personally, I believe Truth is beautiful, inspiring and can help guide our thinking and efforts toward fulfilment - also, the more we understand e.g .others' truths, the more we can redesign society to enable thrival of *all*)
		- Our pursuits of thrival can allow us to pursue truth - we will be materially better off, more energy to devote to uncovering Truth
- There are different routes to these roots
- The ultimate Root is the realisation of Self - it is the continuous pursuit of self-consciousness, elevating this self-consciousness
