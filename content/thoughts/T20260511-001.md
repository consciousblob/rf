title: productivity saturation
---
Something I've noticed is a tendency to feel completely uncompelled to do certain stuff and work when I've already done a certain amount - it's like the more I get done, the more I feel I have license to not get more done. It's like a reach a level of saturation/subconscious satisfaction. But I need to learn how to push through this. Perhaps it's about increasing my threshold of work/activity so that the amount I currently feel is "productive" is really quite miniscule and pathetic compared to the amount I'll end up becoming accustomed to.
