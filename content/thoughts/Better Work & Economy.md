I find the idea of "running out of work" to be preposterous. If AI is able to replace all current work, what does that say about how meaningful this work is? We need to redefine what work even is/means.

There are 2 categories of work:
- Means to an end (for improving efficiency, producing basic necessities/essentials, instrumental, pertains to resources, enabling factors)
- End in itself (for giving meaning/purpose in life, doing it for the sake of it, enjoying the process, finding it fulfilling)

I guess this could be reformulated as:
- "means work"
- "meaning work"

**Means work** is the work most of us are currently engaged in. It is a means to an end. For most of us, it's means to making an income - a "living" - to sustain ourselves and support our lifestyle (from subsistence to luxury). #tbc

Work that is collectively meaningful is work that is both a means to our collectively identified ends (e.g. providing essentials we need to have a good lifestyle within material/ecological bounds), and meaningful to us as individuals.

Ultimately, I think a lot of the work we frame as work *for a living* should be moved into the sphere of leisure.
