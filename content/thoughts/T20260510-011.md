title: MW - question/criticism accumulator
---
Often, people say things I disagree with, or may agree with, but have questions about, or just don't know enough about to form an opinion on. And in all of these cases, I want to develop my views on the things. For example, if someone says something I disagree with but I don't yet have the thoughts formed to be able to explain why, I want to be able to record that thing, and go away and think about it (which may involve research/inputting).
