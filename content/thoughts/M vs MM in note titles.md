I'm trialling a naming convention for notes where:
- The prepend "M - " represents notes pertaining to mindscaping / digital gardening in general.
- The prepend "MM - " represents notes pertaining to *my* mindscape (this site) in particular.

But, the above are also represented by tagging with:
- [[mindscaping]]
- [[my-mindscape]]

Will trial a combination of these because e.g. useful to use the prepend if I forget to add the relevant tag, and makes it easier to see what notes are about at a glance, but could also clutter note titles (so, has potential to both increase and decrease readability). Will see what approach emerges best. I'm guessing I'll abandon these prepends eventually (or soon) but I'll give them a chance and see if they can be useful.
