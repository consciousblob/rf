Exposed.
Let me escape
From myself and my woes.
Let me dig a hole
And climb in carefully -
Close the hatchet,
Until everyone forgets me.
Then, I can re-emerge:
Reborn, renewed -
A brand-spanking-new
Updated 2.0 version of
A human
That internally combusted and malfunctioned and wanted
The off-button.
But I don’t have one.
Please install one?
But I can. I must.
I wait, I sit, I smile.
Stationary, staring, unmoving -
Waiting for the moment when
It all melts away,
Flesh to bone,
Bone to dust
To atom to atom to atom to…
Quark.

Quack!
A duck is born.
Ugly duckling to majestic
Prawn.
To mouth, to
Sea. See what he can see,
Through his one-eyed squint
Of cynicism,
Munching, lunching -
On an item he examines but does not create -
On his own, at least.
But don’t we all create? My body will be
Another body will be
Another body
A…

Body
Of water, gushing through a
Rock - rotating rock,
In a place in space,
In a time in a
Wineglass, hourglass,
Grain to grain to atom to subatom.
Again, again, again,
From one of the smallest - the smallest in existence -
The smallest that contained the seeds of the largest;
The seed of all there is -
The seed of sentimental sentience,
And everything else…
Singularity.
So dull, so sparkling like
Stardust. Dust again.

Hello dust, it’s me again ;)
