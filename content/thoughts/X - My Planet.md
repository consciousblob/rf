My planet: Theia.

Everyone is rather "intense". There is no dithering. Minimal if any "small talk". When we encounter one another, we immediately launch into discussing the most prominent things on our mind as of late, and the things we consider most important and interesting in life. Most of our discussions pertain to explaining and evolving/developing our view on the meaning of life, and general foundational/integral and derivative aspects of our worldview.

But we also love a good laugh and find humour in pretty much anything - for example, a leaf falling on someone's head.

There are silent areas, quiet areas and party/rave areas. There is no in-between. If we feel pensive, a need to recover, retreat, rejuvenate, or just delve into our own mindal world, we go to our independent spaces. Everyone has a large "room" to themselves (their independent space) which is mostly reserved for them and very few others ever enter it - it is seen as an honour to be invited into someone's independent space. There are many public spaces too, for individuals that have pre-bonded (they share some kind of history, they know one another, they have agreed to share a space) - these are the "shared spaces". When you feel particularly energised (have energy to release), you go to a "rave cave" or a "party place". Rave caves are usually dark, enclosed (indoors), like nightclub. Party places are usually out in the open, such as in a wide field/meadow, in a forest, etc. Music is playing loudly for a shared auditory experience, but you also have the ability to put in your complete-noise-cancelling earphones/headphones and listen to your own tunes (silent disco-style). You can take along your compact, portable studio which includes everything you require/desire for creating/materialising, such as a computer, an easel, a DJ deck, etc. (though if you're making music, you have to create a subnet so that only those who want to tap into your creations can hear it, to preserve input autonomy). Everyone dances *crazy style*. Or any style they like. You can just quietly bob along, or you can do full-force breakdance, parkour, raving lunatic stuff, elegant ballet, tribal ritual -style dance, robo-moves, or just running and jumping around haphazardly, fast-walking or just walking - whatever you feel like. There is a "still space" (if your movements are relatively stationary/static) and a "dynamic space" (if your movements are more extensive/chaotic).
