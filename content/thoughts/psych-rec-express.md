==Warning:== some self-piteous, pretty egoic (via glorification of past self) brain-dumps below.

---
Wavy aura swirling and twirling
On the motorbike of physical experience
Action manifest
In human form
Divine being - what is your name?
Who are you?
Souls holding hands
Around the black hole of the galaxy,
Rotating, gyrating,
Singing, smiling
You thought you saw it all.
But as quickly as it came,
You became
A loon.
Oh dark night,
When will you yield?
And reveal
All the colours I once saw
And more?
When will you present
Your secrets to me?
And unveil the mind
I once had,
But better?

---
Opinions.
You share yours
Willy nilly
Prepare to look silly.

---
Sit forward,
Stand back
And bask in all that
You lack.
Meanwhile,
Another rolls in love -
Self-love,
And attracts
More love.
Your presence reminds me
Of what is not present
Within me.
Your aura instils in me
A sense of lack.
Fill my cup?
Nope, I’ll do it myself.

---
Study the scriptures, bloody the soul
Torn to shreds is a pack of
Thoughts - a tapestry of
Beliefs I once held
So dear.
All I grew to admire,
Now holds me in a mire,
As my mind torments itself
With lost traits, attributes to grieve.
Creativity, focus,
Curiosity, discipline -
Are these not the markers
Of a sound mind?
A mind which generates such
Stunning, sonorous frequencies?
And where can I find these attributes?
Claim them, as one stakes a flag in the sand,
In the grains which preceded and will succeed
The placer of the flag?
Oh, sound mind - 
Where are you?
Where is the fluttering, floating, freely fabulous
Little self, fluxating around a point of
Excellency, vibrancy, brilliancy?
Where is my mind?

---
From oasis to puddle,
Geez - my head is in a muddle.
I just think about how I’m not thinking,
Cling desperately to past insights
They flutter and flicker before my mind’s eye,
Trying to regain the speed of thought by
Following a train of thought -
Picking a thread and pulling on it
Until it separates from the mainchain
Running on past merit -
The products of a past intelligence
One day, we’ll all lose our minds,
So make the most of yours while you can.

---
Languid mind, languid soul -
Trapped. Behind a glass window,
Peering into the past,
When I saw the law of language,
And when the universe listened to itself,
Through me.
When I swirled into the ground,
And things became beings - became me,
Because everything became everything,
And the harmonies of life aligned
Into a neat package in the core of my mind,
My soul.
When I saw the integrated Whole,
And its differentiated parts,
Pulsating as the One
Entity that we are.

---
Will this mind
Generate; create; relate;
Ever again?
Will this soul
Expand; express; ignite;
Ever again?
Unfeeling. Dampened. Dissolved. Devolved.
Will this person
Be free to evolve
Ever again?
Or are they condemned
To a life of stifled stagnation?

---
Your smile elevates us.
When you gaze upon me,
I graze on the grass of your love.
I live in a state of restlessness -
Tormented skies, swirling danger
Confronting emptiness in its eye -
An eye that sees haze,
With no clear sky
I just don’t want to die,
No -
I want to feel alive
Empty. Madness. Chaos. Confusion.
No rest, no peace.
Why? WHY? Where did you go?
I’m trying to find you
Help me out here
Where are you?
What is this? What is the point? What do I contribute?

---
Time heals, time kills.
Time gives and takes
All of life’s thrills.
Time softens all your mistakes.
Time equalises.
Time tranquilises.
Time erodes life and lump,
Mind and matter,
In spite of your quantum jump.
Time overlooks all the world’s chatter.
Time is the neutral arbiter
Of life
And death.

---
You entered me.
I wanted to kill.
Angelic demon or demonic angel?
Doesn’t matter -
Demon either way.
Watched me as I cried
For a time when you
Murdered me,
And hid the evidence.
Laughed as my flesh melted
From my bones
And the stench of decay
Filled our nostrils.
Screamed stiff and sang melodically,
Through me.
I thought I was the one.
Imbued me with
Your ‘strength’.
I didn’t need it.
I didn’t want it.
You didn’t care.
Ripped through me,
Like a horde of worms.
I ran outside, in the rain,
Barefoot on the stones
To kill you.
Did it work?
Do you linger in
My mind, my soul -
Feeding off my fears,
Fighting my recovery?

Your grip will loosen,
As my frequency heightens.
Goodbye, demon -
Into the light (of fire) you go.

---
Limited mind,
Just be kind.
They’ll like you if
You’re not so stiff.
Just nod and smile,
And for a while,
They’ll think you’re nice,
Though you lack some spice.

Limited mind,
Don’t look behind.
Just walk on ahead,
And like they said:
This too shall pass
Just touch some grass
And smell the soil -
Forget the turmoil.
All will be well,
Though right now, that’s hard to sell.


---
Beneath the scratch
Is a scar, so far
You can’t see the beginning,
Nor the end.

Beneath the scar is
A stagnant lake
Brimming with parasites,
Swimming with murk.

Beneath the lake is
A crusty crust -
A non-bedrock base
Of blankness and bore.

Beneath the crust is
A fiery glacier -
Magma meandering,
Awaiting eruption.

Beneath the glacier is
A solid core -
The stateless state within which
All other states occur.

Dormant. Waiting.
If only you knew,
The fire it cradles
Down in the depths
Of Self,
Waiting to burst forth
And shower the world
With fresh fumes of ferocious force -
Life force.
We are alive!

---
Always the same,
But never the same again.
A one-time fleeting appearance
As a character in a show with
Seemingly different plots,
Which on closer reflection,
Reveal patterns - cycles,
Some of which become swirls:
Upwards to integration,
Or downwards to disintegration.
But ultimately,
In the end,
All will spiral toward the existential skies of time,
And in time,
All will explicitly reveal the Whole,
Which is implicit in all its little parts -
In the parts we play
On this Earthly stage. 

---
Paralysed.
Staring, never starting.
Waiting for the end,
Or for reality to bend
Again -
To straighten to what was.
Lamenting past in present.
Watching my blood pumping.
Feeling alive outside, but dead inside.
Feeling the cold lick my legs,
Reminding me I’m not dead yet.
When will it end?
This paralysis, or this life?
I just want it to end.
When will I wake up, or cease to sleep?

I remember when I felt the universe inside me,
Outside me,
When I heard the universe in everything,
When I saw it all.
