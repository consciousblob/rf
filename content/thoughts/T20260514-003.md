[[T20260511-013]]

[[integration|differentiation]] (in preferences, abilities, etc.) is inherent to this physical dimension.

The world can be thought of as a collection of many overlapping "realms" (or spheres, dimensions, terrains), where each realm represents a particular area of competency/innate ability. For each individual, there exists a realm in which they are the *best* individual. They exceed, excel, dominate. They are the guardian of that realm. Now, because of the overlap of the realms, and depending on the nature of the realm, there will be at least some individuals with very similar spheres of competence. But there will always be some distinction/difference, in which you can claim ultimate authority in that particular region of the collective competency terrain. And in life, it's your role to find that spot, and build in it. Find the pedestal, get up on it, and start conducting your own orchestra, giving your speeches, etc. But you can only hope to locate your pedestal if you never definitively declare that you've found it (basic root-finding principle).
