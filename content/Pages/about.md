I'm trialling different ways of explaining what this site even is. But at this point, I don't even really know what is is, so it'll be quite difficult to explain effectively... Anyway, here are some attempts:

- The basic, relatively convergent (i.e., concise, traditionally understandable) [[index|Home]] page
- A more divergent attempt through the lens of "GaGa": [[about-gaga]]

**Ultimately:** In its initial, embryonic, very nebulous/chaotic state, this site exists primarily for me. It is not at this stage supposed to be completely coherent / make complete sense / be easy to navigate/follow for anyone else, because if I restrict myself to only outputting stuff that meets this criteria, I won't be able to maximally [[diverge-converge]] - and divergence is a high priority for me right now.

That being said, the eventual point of expressing my thoughts is for them to be relevant/useful to other minds, and to participate in continuous global discussions about things I care about, with individuals who also care about these things. So, I need to be able to communicate my thoughts effectively. And for this, some kind of structure/organisation is necessary. So, below is my first attempt at outlining a structure.

# Site Structure
How this site is currently organised.

## General

### Notes
The basic unit of this site is a "note", which could represent anything from a relatively *underdeveloped* thought (e.g. a singular statement) to a relatively *developed* collection of thoughts (e.g. this About page).

### Nebula sections
Notes will often start with a "Nebula" section, which contains a list of thoughts that I want to slot somewhere in the note, but haven't yet decided where exactly to place them. Once thought clusters emerge in this nebula - i.e., I feel they gravitate toward (relate to) each other - and I feel ready to merge them, I'll pull them out into the "main body" of the note: the (proto)star forming to illuminate whatever thing the note pertains to.

To reiterate the organic sister-analogy to this cosmic analogy (discussed in [[about-gaga]]), the Nebula section is like compost, and the rest of the note is like a plant growing from it. Another organic/ecological comparison: the Nebula section holds a random-but-relevant jungle(/jumble) of thoughts, and the note's main body is like a civilisation / built environment around the jungle.

Note: I previously used the term "To Integrate" instead of "Nebula", so some notes will have this old label until I update them.

### Pages
"Pages" are a particular kind of note that could be thought of as a hub for many thoughts/notes. This page is one example. In the following subsections, I'll outline the other main pages for this site.

### Playing with pronouns
I sometimes (well, often) use pronouns such as "TM" and "TE" instead of "I". This note talks about why: [[T vs TE vs TB vs TM (self & other -referencing)]].

### Tags
There are a lot of #tags floating around, and I just sprinkle them whenever I feel like it, making up new ones as I feel inclined to, with the goal of, over time, converging on more standardised usage. But I want to this standardisation to be *emergent* not *enforced* by pre-determining what tags will do, etc.

The [[about-gaga]] page discusses a lot of tags and their intended usage, but I wrote that a good few months ago from when I'm writing this (on [[Wed 12-08-2026]]), so my approach to organising this mindscape is quite different. But, a lot of the things I discussed are probably still relevant.

One tag I seem to be using a lot lately is #dc, which is explained a bit more here: [[Tags - dc vs expand]].


## My Worldview
I use the term "my worldview" to represent my current set of views on existence - i.e., what I conceive of everything I perceive to exist - and the [[my-worldview|My Worldview]] page holds my latest attempt at explaining this worldview.

Since a worldview comprises *many* things that are difficult to obtain an exhaustive list of, I don't think I'm currently able to "explain" my worldview comprehensively on a single page. So, I focus only on discussing thoughts/concepts/beliefs/ideas that are particularly prominent in my worldview - as in, I think of them often, and often reference them in other thoughts (i.e., they're foundational (roots) to other parts of my worldview).

## Blobs
A blob is basically any output in any form. It's represents a blob of thought substance. Blobs will be categorised via:
- Medium (text, image, audio, video, mixed) [^medium]
- Form (essay/article, note, story, poem, diagram, etc.)
- Purpose (exploratory, expressive)

A blob may not be defined in/by all categories. I'll refine the categorisation system as I use it. For now, most blobs are mostly undefined.

[^medium]: **Image** could represent a picture/photo, piece of (attempted) art, diagram, etc. **Audio** could be a recording (unlikely as I'd find that especially cringey to release, but possible), an attempted piece of music (very aspirational - I'd love to create music, but currently have 0 materialisation skills here). **Video** would mostly be vids from TM's [YT channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ABlobofConsciousness)  though there's perhaps no need to "store" them here (create dedicated blobs on this site for them) for now. **Mixed** is anything that could combine any of the other media; I guess "video" is a combination of image and audio, but it's distinct and ubiquitous enough to have its own category. **I also want to emphasise:** I might not make substantial use of this medium property on notes for now, because with current mindware, it's quite difficult to atomise thought to the extent that explicitly labelling a blob's medium is useful. I mainly mention/introduce this property for its utility in future mindware developments, because it would be useful for translating between media (e.g. you output a thought via video, I input it via text).
