Here, I would like to elaborate on what "Root-Finding" (which I'll often shorten to RF) means in the context of this site and [[my-worldview]] more generally.

I think of there being 5 "kinds" or subsets of roots:

- R0: **existence**
	- What exists?
- R1: **roots of existence**
	- Why/how does it exist?
- R2: **roots of our existence**
	- Why/how do we exist? [^1]
- R3: **roots of our good existence**
	- What is a good way to exist? [^2]
- R4: **routes[^3] to our good existence**
	- How can we have a good existence?

Here, "our" has a double meaning: it refers to us as (1) a collection of individuals and (2) a collective. So, the three root subsets containing "our" could be considered as:

- R2: **roots of our existence**
	- Why/how do you, as an individual being, exist? How have your unique natural (genetic) and [[nurtural]] (environmental) circumstances contributed to your current state of existence (i.e., way of being, worldview, character, values, etc.)? 
	- Why/how have we, as a collective (collection of individuals), arrived at this current state of existence (i.e., structure of society / particular societal configuration)?
- R3: **roots of our good existence**
	- What is your personal definition of "good" - what is a good life/society?
	- What is our collective definition of "good" [^4], and what would a good society full of people leading good lives look like?
- R4: **routes[^3] to our good existence**
	- According to your definition of good, how can you as an individual have a good life? What thoughts, mindsets, actions, etc. are required to have a good life?
	- How can we consciously act as individuals within the collective to reorganise ourselves in pursuit of a good society which allows us all to have good lives?

So, RF is supposed to represent both a theoretical and practical methodology/framework, for investigating life and doing life, inspired/informed by those investigations.

By this definition/delineation of RF, there exist and have existed many RFers (root-finders) throughout space and time on this planet[^planet-spacetime]. Root-finders are curious about the world, want to have a good life, and constantly seek to understand more about the world/life and how to co-create/have a good one - i.e., how to contribute to restructuring society in a way that allows them (and others) to have a better life while simultaneously taking actions that make them feel like they're leading a good life individually.

If this is how you tend to approach things/life, then you are a root-finder. Welcome :)
