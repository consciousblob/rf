Data
Information
Knowledge
Wisdom

Layers of abstraction. Hierarchy of meaning.

- **Data** is raw input (all available input)
- **Information** is a meaningful synthesis of data
- **Knowledge** is a meaningful synthesis of information
- **Wisdom** is a meaningful synthesis of knowledge

where "meaningful synthesis of X" means a collapse/condensation of X into something that would be considered more useful (meaningful) than the unsynthesised fragments.
